## 摘要
We establish sufficient condition 用于 ultimate positivity 的 P-recursive sequences 的 arbitrary order 结。

核心做法：By additionally verifying finitely many initial terms，positivity can also be resolved。

主要结果：As application，we provide several examples 的 P-recursive sequences 的 order greater 相比 two。

值得关注的点在于：这篇文章的价值在于把《结合unique dominant root的arbitrary-order P-recursive sequences的。

## Abstract
We establish a sufficient condition for the ultimate positivity of P-recursive sequences of arbitrary order with a unique dominant root. By additionally verifying finitely many initial terms, the positivity can also be resolved. As an application, we provide several examples of P-recursive sequences of order greater than two.
