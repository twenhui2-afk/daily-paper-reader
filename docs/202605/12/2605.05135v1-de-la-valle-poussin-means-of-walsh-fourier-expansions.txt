> USHANGI GOGINAVA
> Abstract. We study de la Vallée Poussin means of Walsh–Fourier series associated with a nondecreasing window sequence. We establish a sharp criterion for almost everywhere convergence for integrable functions. We further show that, when this criterion fails, every Orlicz class below the logarithmic square-root scale contains a function whose de la Vallée Poussin means diverge everywhere.

1. Introduction 

Questions on convergence and divergence of Fourier series in function spaces remain central in harmonic analysis. For trigonometric Fourier series, Kolmogorov proved the existence of an integrable function with almost everywhere divergent Fourier series [9, 10], while Marcinkiewicz constructed an example with bounded divergence almost ev-erywhere [20]. Further divergence results in Orlicz classes were obtained by Prokhorenko [22], Tandori [28], Lukashenko [19], Körner [15, 16], Gosselin [7], Totik [30], Konyagin [12, 13, 14], Heladze [8], Gát [2]. The classical problem posed by Zygmund asks whether the Orlicz class generated by the function t 7 → t log + t guarantees almost everywhere convergence of Fourier series; this is still open [31, Ch. XIII]. Parallel problems have been investigated for the Walsh–Paley system. Stein [27] proved the existence of an integrable function whose Walsh–Fourier series diverges ev-erywhere. Divergence in Orlicz classes close to L1 was established by Moon [21], Schipp [24], and Simon [26]; see also Bochkarev, [1] Konyagin [11], Gát, Goginava, Karagulyan, Mukhamedov, Oniani [3, 4, 5, 6].
