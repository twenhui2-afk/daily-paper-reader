## 摘要
two Blanuša snarks B_1 与 B_2 are 3-regular graphs on 18 vertices。

主要结果：We pull upper bounds down to π(B_1) \le 28 与 π(B_2) \le 29。

## Abstract
The two Blanuša snarks $B_1$ and $B_2$ are 3-regular graphs on 18 vertices. Dantas, Lordelo, Niedermaier and Nogueira (Discrete Appl.\ Math.\ 361, 2025, pp.~336--346) established $23 \le π(B_i) \le 34$ for $i=1,2$, leaving a gap of 11 in each case. We pull the upper bounds down to $π(B_1) \le 28$ and $π(B_2) \le 29$. The route is Hurlbert's Weight Function Lemma, applied one orbit at a time; the optimal weight functions come from a linear program over a corpus of roughly $30{,}000$ rooted-subtree strategies per target. For the lower bound $π(B_i) \ge 23$ we re-derive the witnesses of Dantas et al.\ and re-verify them with two independent oracles: an exhaustive forward state-space search, and a sound-and-complete MILP encoding whose acyclicity constraint is motivated by the Milans--Clark No-Cycle Lemma. The published gaps $[23, 34]$ thus shrink to $[23, 28]$ for $B_1$ and $[23, 29]$ for $B_2$.
