1.1. An explicit construction, including a dimension formula, for the irreducible repre-sentations of symmetric groups Sn in module case over an algebraically closed field F of characteristic p > 0 is still an open important problem. A landmark result in this direction was obtained by Mathieu [M], who computed the dimensions of the irreducible modules associated to partitions λ = ( λ1, . . . , λ l) of n with l = ℓ(λ) satisfying λ1 − λl + l ≤ p,by means of the classical Schur-Weyl duality. Kleshchev [K1] later characterized these modules representation theoretically by showing they coincide precisely with those whose restriction to every subgroup Sk for all 1 ≤ k < n is semisimple, or equivalently, on which the Jucys-Murphy elements of FSn act semisimply. Following [K1], such modules are called completely splittable (also called calibrated or homogeneous in literature). Us-ing the modular branching rules for Sn (cf. [K2]), one then obtains a dimension formula in terms of paths in Young modular graphs, which recovers Mathieu’s result.

Theorem 6.11. The Hecke-Clifford superalgebra Hn(q) is semisimple if and only if h > n 

in the case h is odd and h > 2n in the case h is even. Proof. In the case h is odd, if h > n , then Hn(q) is semisimple by Proposition 6.8. Now suppose h ≤ n. Then Hh(q) is not semisimple by Proposition 6.8 and hence 

Hh+1 (q), Hh+2 (q), . . . are not semisimple by Theorem 6.10. Therefore Hn(q) is not semisim-ple. The same discussion holds in the case h being even. □HECKE-CLIFFORD ALGEBRAS 41 

Remark 6.12 . Theorem 6.11 verifies the equivalence between (1) and (3) in [SW, Con-jecture 4.13] for Hn(q). Meanwhile by Proposition 6.8 one can see that (2) is neither equivalent to (1) nor (3) in [SW, Conjecture 4.13] for Hn(q). In [Sh], a different method by extending the trace form introduced in[WW] to cyclotomic Hecke superalgebras and computing the associated Schur elements is applied to derive the necessary condition for cyclotomic Hecke superalgebras including Hn(q) as a special case to be semisimple under certain assumptions. Our statement shows that the assumption in the case of Hn(q) in [Sh, Theorem 1.3(2)] can be removed.
