## Abstract
Fluid models offer crucial computational efficiency for plasma simulations, yet accurately capturing kinetic effects like Landau damping remains a fundamental challenge. While conventional closures (e.g., Hammett-Perkins and Hunana) are widely used, their fidelity relative to exact kinetic simulations degrades significantly depending on the perturbation wave number. Here, we propose a novel wave-number-dependent closure condition for the three-moment fluid equations that explicitly preserves the primary dispersion relation. By mapping Padé approximant coefficients directly to the kinetic roots of the collisionless Vlasov-Poisson system, we derive an analytical closure that dynamically adapts to specific wave numbers. We further demonstrate that this framework readily extends to collisional plasmas via the BGK model. Numerical benchmarks confirm that this approach substantially improves the long-term accuracy of electric field energy and fluid moment evolution, offering a potential paradigm shift for high-fidelity fluid modeling.
