## Abstract
For the fixed-size Brualdi--Hoffman--Turan problem forbidding the fish graph $H(4,3)$, Zheng and Zhang proved that every $H(4,3)$-free graph of even size $m \ge 38$ without isolated vertices satisfies $ρ(G) \le ρ'(m)$, where $ρ'(m)$ is the largest root of $x^4-mx^2-(m-2)x+\frac{m}{2}-1=0$, with equality only for $S^{-}_{\frac{m+4}{2},2}$. We revisit their branch analysis and show that the published threshold is carried entirely by one local configuration, namely the branch in which the Perron-neighborhood core $A^{+}$ induces $K_4$. By refining that branch, we lower the unconditional threshold from $38$ to $24$. The cases $e(W)=0$ and $e(W)=1$ are resolved exactly, while the case $e(W)=2$ remains the bottleneck and yields the final hand-proved bound. We also exhibit an explicit obstruction family showing that no theorem of the same form can hold below $18$, and we record computational evidence that the exact threshold is likely $18$.
