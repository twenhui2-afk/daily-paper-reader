## Abstract
Following \cite {KSW} we continue the study the cellular complexes formed by polynomials of a given degree having a given sequence of multiplicities of real roots.   A computer-assisted calculation disproves the earlier conjecture that homology of one point compactification of the closure of such cell is concentrated in at most one degree. Namely, for $ω=(3,1,1,3)$ in degree $d=18$, the reduced homology is $\ZZ^2$ in degree $7$. The obstruction is already visible in a signed cell count, whose value is $-2$.   We relate that count to the rational signed weight enumerator $F_ω(t)=\sum_{η\preceqω}(-1)^{\elln(η)}t^{|η|}$. For the counterexample, $F_ω(t)=-t^{14}/(1+t^2)^2$, which gives an exact linear formula for the Euler characteristic and forces the total rational Betti number to be unbounded.   We prove a number of results and formulate a complete conjecture describing the above homology.
