## Abstract
Heretofore, the second and third author conjectured that the D'Arcais polynomials, related to the coefficients of the powers of the Dedekind $η$-function, are Hurwitz polynomials except for a root at the origin. We show that this does in fact not hold for a positive proportion of all natural numbers.
