## 摘要
Let L_2=\mathbb{Z}α be rank-one 根格结合 (α,α)=2，与 let A_5 act on 格 vertex operator algebra V_{L_2} thro。

核心做法：We prove that fixed-point vertex operator algebra V_{L_2}^{A_5} is C_2-cofinite 与 strongly rational。

主要结果：摘要给出了正向结果。

值得关注的点在于：这篇文章的价值在于把《Icosahedral Orbifold V_{L_2}^{A_5}的C_2-Cofiniteness与Rationali。

## Abstract
Let $L_2=\mathbb{Z}α$ be the rank-one root lattice with $(α,α)=2$, and let $A_5$ act on the lattice vertex operator algebra $V_{L_2}$ through an icosahedral subgroup of $\operatorname{Aut}(V_{L_2})\cong PSL_2(\mathbb{C})$. We prove that the fixed-point vertex operator algebra $V_{L_2}^{A_5}$ is $C_2$-cofinite and strongly rational.
