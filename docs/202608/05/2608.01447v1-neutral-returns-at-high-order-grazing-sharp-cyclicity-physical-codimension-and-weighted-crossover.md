## Abstract
A neutral return and an even-order grazing contact can share one orbit of a continuous piecewise-smooth flow. Let \(n\geq2\) be the first nonzero order of the smooth return displacement and \(ν\geq2\) the grazing order. On the fixed-order stratum, continuity produces a ramified correction. If \(c\) and \(d\) are the leading smooth and ramified coefficients, the sharp number of small isolated fixed points is \(n\) for \(cd>0\) and \(n+1\) for \(cd<0\). If the smooth coefficient map has rank \(n\), the corresponding bound is attained. Thus fixed-stratum cyclicity is independent of \(ν\). Under Lie-contact-jet transversality, the order-\(ν\) grazing stratum has ambient codimension \(ν-2\); if the restricted smooth coefficient map has rank \(n\), simultaneous neutral grazing has codimension \(n+ν-2\). Transverse perturbations are governed by a weighted-homogeneous positive-part cap. Its onset crosses from exponent \(1+1/ν\) at the central graze to the \(3/2\) law at each fixed sufficiently small nonzero tilt in a generic linear direction. Both the exact cap and prepared physical single-well passages with derivative control preserve the sharp root bound; the value-only counterexample shows that derivative control is essential. A closed planar family realizes the ranks and sharp central configurations. Contact order changes geometric cost and crossover scale, while fixed-stratum cyclicity stays rigid.
