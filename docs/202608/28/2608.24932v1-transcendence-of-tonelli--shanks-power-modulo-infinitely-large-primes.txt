Tomoki Mihara 

Abstract 

For a prime number p, we denote by kp the greatest odd divisor of p−1. Tonelli– Shanks algorithm is a classical algorithm to compute a square root of a quadratic residue n ∈ Z modulo p as the product of n kp +12 , which we call Tonelli–Shanks power of n , and a correction term given as a power of a quadratic non-residue modulo p.We prove the transcendence over Q of the images of the following in the ring A

# 0 Introduction 

We denote by P the set of prime numbers. Let p ∈ P. We denote by kp ∈ N the greatest odd divisor of p − 1. For k ∈ 2N + 1, we set Pk ⊂ { p ∈ P \ { 2} | kp = k}. It is di ffi cult to 1arXiv:2608.24932v1 [math.NT] 23 Aug 2026 determine # Pk for k ∈ N, and there are several number theoretic conjectures related to its infiniteness. 

Conjecture 0.1 (Fermat Prime Conjecture) . There exist infinitely many Fermat primes, i.e. the equation #P1 = ∞ holds. 

Conjecture 0.2 (Thabit Prime Conjecture) . There exist infinitely many Thabit primes of second kind, i.e. the equation #P3 = ∞ holds. 

There is no known k ∈ 2N+1 with # Pk = ∞. Indeed, the existence of such a k directly implies Proth Prime Conjecture. 

Conjecture 0.3 (Proth Prime Conjecture) . There exist infinitely many Proth primes, i.e. p ∈ P with k p < pp − 1.

Compared to the infiniteness problem of # Pk for k ∈ N, the emptiness problem has ever been successfully studied. 

Theorem 0.4 ([Sie60] Théorème) . There exist infinitely many Sierpi´ nski numbers, i.e. k ∈ 2N + 1 with #Pk = 0.

Conjecture 0.5 (Sierpi´ nski conjecture) . The least Sierpi´ nski number is 78557 .

Theorem 0.6 ([EO79] Theorem 1) . There exist infinitely many odd numbers which are not Sierpi´ nski numbers, i.e. k ∈ 2N + 1 with #Pk > 0.
