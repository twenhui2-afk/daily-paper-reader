# Lattice vertex algebras of type ADE over fields of prime characteristic and their representations 

Qiang Mu ∗ and Hongju Zhao School of Mathematical Sciences, Harbin Normal University, Harbin 150025, China 

Abstract 

We study lattice vertex algebras of type ADE over an algebraically closed field F of prime characteristic p > 2 and their representations. Let L be a root lattice of type ADE, and GL the Gram matrix of L. When det GL̸ ≡ 0 (mod p), we establish an isomorphism between the lattice vertex algebra VL, F and the level-one simple affine vertex algebra of the same type. Via this isomorphism, we classify the irreducible N-graded modules of 

VL, F viewed as an N-graded vertex algebra. We also consider the case where L is of type 

An with det GL = n + 1 ≡ 0 (mod p). We show that VL, F is not simple and determine the simple N-graded quotient of VL, F. Furthermore, when n + 1 = ap for some a ∈ Z+

with gcd( a, p ) = 1, we give the classification of the irreducible N-graded modules for the simple quotient of VL, F.

# 1 Introduction 

Vertex (operator) algebras associated with even lattices are among the most important objects in vertex algebra theory and provide many fundamental examples (see, for example, [D, DG, DLM, FLM, LW]). For instance, the well-known Moonshine vertex operator algebra V ♮ is constructed from the vertex operator algebra associated to the Leech lattice (see [FLM]). Let L be a positive definite even lattice with a basis {γ1, . . . , γ d} and a bilinear form ⟨· , ·⟩ .Over a field of characteristic zero, one can construct the lattice vertex operator algebra VL

associated with L, called the lattice vertex operator algebra. However, this approach does not directly extend to fields of prime characteristic. The integral form provides a common tool for passing from characteristic zero to prime characteristic. For example, in [DG], Dong and Griess introduced an integral form of the lattice vertex operator algebra VL, denoted by VL, Z.Subsequently, Mu studied lattice vertex algebras over fields F of prime characteristic p obtained from VL, Z, namely VL, F := VL, Z ⊗Z F, and proved that if det GL̸ ≡ 0 (mod p), then VL, F is a simple vertex operator algebra (see [M]). Here, GL = ( ⟨γi, γ j ⟩) is the Gram matrix of L.Note that the lattice vertex algebra of type ADE is isomorphic to the level-one simple affine vertex algebra of the same type over a field of characteristic zero (see, for example, [K]). This isomorphism allows one to investigate the representation theory of lattice vertex algebras via that of affine vertex algebras. Motivated by this, we study lattice vertex algebras of type ADE over fields of prime characteristic and their representations, building on the results of [DG] and [M].
