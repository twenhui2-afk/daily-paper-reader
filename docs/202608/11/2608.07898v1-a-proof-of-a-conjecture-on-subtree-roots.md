## Abstract
The subtree polynomial of a tree is the generating function that enumerates its subtrees according to their orders. Brown and Mol conjectured that every subtree root of a tree of order \(n\ge 2\) lies in the disk \[ \left\{z\in\mathbb C: |z|\le 1+\sqrt[n-1]{n-1} \right\}. \] We confirm this conjecture. We also show that every nonzero subtree root \(z\) satisfies \[ |z|>\sqrt[n-1]{n-1}-1. \] Consequently, all nonzero subtree roots lie in the annular region   \[ \left\{z\in\mathbb C: \sqrt[n-1]{n-1}-1 < |z| \le 1+\sqrt[n-1]{n-1} \right\}. \] The upper bound is sharp for every even \(n\) and is asymptotically sharp as \(n\to\infty\) through odd integers. The lower bound is also asymptotically sharp as \(n\to\infty\).
