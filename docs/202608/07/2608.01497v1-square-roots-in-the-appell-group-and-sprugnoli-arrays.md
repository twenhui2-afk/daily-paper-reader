## 摘要
作者引入 special mapping from pairs 的 power series to group 的 Sprugnoli matrices。

核心做法：This mapping has property when second argument is even power series，then square 的 resulting Sprugnol。

主要结果：This allows us to explore square roots 的 elements in aerated Appell subgroup。

值得关注的点在于：As identity is element 的 this subgroup，we are led to explore related involutions in Sprugnoli group。

## Abstract
We introduce a special mapping from pairs of power series to the group of Sprugnoli matrices. This mapping has the property when the second argument is an even power series, then the square of the resulting Sprugnoli array is an aerated element of the Appell subgroup of the Riordan group. This allows us to explore the square roots of elements in the aerated Appell subgroup. As the identity is an element of this subgroup, we are led to explore related involutions in the Sprugnoli group.
