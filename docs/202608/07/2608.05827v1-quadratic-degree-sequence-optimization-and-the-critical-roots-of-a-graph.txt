# Quadratic Degree Sequence Optimization and the Critical Roots of a Graph 

Fr´ ed´ eric Meunier ∗ Shmuel Onn †‡ 

Abstract 

The degree sequence optimization problem is to find a subgraph of a given graph which maximizes the sum over all vertices of a given function evaluated at the subgraph degree of that vertex. Here we study this problem and its complexity for quadratic functions. In par-ticular, we introduce the critical roots of a graph , and show they define intervals over which the optimal value of the problem, as the quadratic root varies, is convex piecewise affine. 

Keywords: graph, degree sequence, combinatorial optimization, quadratic optimization, convex function, piecewise affine function 

# 1 Introduction 

Let H = ( V, F ) be a given n-graph, that is, with |V | = n vertices, and let f : [ n] → R be a given function, where [ n] := {0, 1, . . . , n − 1}. Here, unless otherwise specified, by a subgraph 

G ⊆ H we mean a subgraph G = ( V, E ) of H with the same vertex set V and a subset of the edges E ⊆ F . We write G = ∅ for the empty subgraph G = ( V, ∅). We denote the degree of vertex v ∈ V in G ⊆ H by dv(G). The function f induces a function on subgraphs by

We are interested in the following optimization problem denoted DSO for brevity. 

Degree Sequence Optimization. Given H = ( V, F ) and f : [ n] → R, solve 

DSO : max {f (G) : G ⊆ H} .

This problem has been studied by several authors, in particular in [1], where the function
