## Abstract
By combining the recent results of Pierce and Xu on the higher-dimensional Burgess bound with the classical one-dimensional Burgess bound, we prove that for any sufficiently large prime $p$, there exists a Pythagorean triple $(a_p,b_p,c_p)$ with $a_p,b_p,c_p\in\mathbb{Z}\cap(0,p)$ such that $a_pb_p/2$ is a primitive root modulo $p$. This confirms a conjecture of Z.-W. Sun for all sufficiently large primes.
