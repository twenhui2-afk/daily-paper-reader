# Residue construction of quantized Coulomb branches 

Daniil Klyuev August 4, 2026 

Abstract 

We describe the image of a quantized BFN Coulomb branch 𝒜 ℏ=1 

> 𝐺,𝑁

under localization (abelianization) map for any 𝐺, 𝑁 . In most cases, such as quiver gauge theories without loops, this description works for any flavors, sometimes we need to take generic or formal flavor param-eters. The answer is given by a roots and residue condition similar to Ginzburg—Kapranov—Vasserot construction of DAHA [GKV]. As a corollary, for any quantized conical Coulomb branch (and zero or small flavors) we provide a mathematical construction of the sphere trace in-troduced by Gaiotto and Okazaki [GO].
