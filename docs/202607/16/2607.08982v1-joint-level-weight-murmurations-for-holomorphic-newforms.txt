JOINT LEVEL–WEIGHT MURMURATIONS FOR HOLOMORPHIC NEWFORMS 

JULIEN CARDI 

Abstract. Let N run over squarefree levels of size X, and let the even weight k run over a window of size K. We prove a joint level–weight limit for the root-number weighted prime trace of holomorphic newforms, uniformly in 

Xε ≤ K ≤ X1−ε.

After the prime is measured in the coordinate p/ (N (( k − 1) /(4 π)) 2),the limiting measure is supported on the rational squares. Its masses are explicit Euler products. The proof uses the exact trace of TpWN .Summation over the weight makes the elliptic parameter in that formula rapidly summable. A uniform class-number estimate can then be proved by truncating quadratic L-values with Burgess’s bound and averaging the resulting periodic functions over squarefree levels. The inequality 

K < X 1−ε is precisely what keeps the largest period below the length of the level interval. No hypothesis on zeros of Dirichlet or automorphic 

L-functions is used. 

1. Introduction 

For a normalized holomorphic newform f of squarefree level N , even weight 

k, and trivial character, write λf (n) for its normalized Hecke eigenvalues and 

ϵf ∈ {± 1} for its root number. Murmurations concern correlations between 

λf (p) and ϵf when the prime p is placed at the scale of the conductor. Zubrilina [ 8] obtained the level-aspect correlation for fixed weight. The weight-aspect correlation at fixed level was studied in [ 2, 4]. We consider the range in which the level and the weight tend to infinity together. This supplies an interior joint regime for the double-limit problem raised in [ 2]; the boundary regimes in which one parameter is kept fixed are not part of the theorem.
