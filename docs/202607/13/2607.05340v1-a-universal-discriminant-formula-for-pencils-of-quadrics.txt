# A universal discriminant formula for pencils of quadrics 

Ari Krishna July 7, 2026 

Abstract 

Let 𝑉 be a vector space of dimension 𝑛 + 1 over an algebraically closed field 𝕜 of characteristic zero, and let 

𝐺 𝑛 = Gr (2, Sym 2 𝑉 ∨)

be the Grassmannian parametrizing pencils of quadrics in ℙ(𝑉 ) ≅ ℙ𝑛 . The determinant of the universal pencil defines a universal binary form of degree 𝑛 + 1 . We prove that the divisor 𝑛 ⊆ 𝐺 𝑛 of pencils whose determinant binary form has a multiple root has Chow class 

[𝑛 ] = 𝑛 (𝑛 + 1) 𝜎 1 ∈ 𝐴 1(𝐺 𝑛 ),

where 𝜎 1 = 𝑐 1(𝑆 ∨) and 𝑆 is the tautological rank-two subbundle on 𝐺 𝑛 . More generally, the higher-contact loci of determinant binary forms are computed by a universal jet formula. We also formulate the determinant-root collision strata as refined pullbacks of the universal collision strata for binary forms. For 𝑛 = 3 , the main formula recovers the class 12 𝜎 1 for the boundary divisor in Gr(2 , 10) that the author established in a prior paper. 

# 1 Introduction 

Let 𝑉 be a vector space of dimension 𝑛 + 1 over an algebraically closed field 𝕜 of characteristic zero. A pencil of quadrics in ℙ(𝑉 ) ≅ ℙ𝑛 is a two-dimensional subspace 

𝐿 ⊆ Sym 2 𝑉 ∨.

After choosing a basis 𝑄 0, 𝑄 1 of 𝐿 , the singular members of the pencil are detected by the vanishing of the determinant 

Δ𝐿 (𝑠, 𝑡 ) = det( 𝑠𝑄 0 + 𝑡𝑄 1).

This is a binary form of degree 𝑛 + 1 , and its roots record the singular quadrics in the pencil, counted with multiplicity. Set 

𝐺 𝑛 ∶= Gr (2, Sym 2 𝑉 ∨).

Let 𝑆 ⊂ Sym 2 𝑉 ∨ ⊗ 𝐺 𝑛 be the tautological rank-two subbundle, and write 

𝜎 1 = 𝑐 1(𝑆 ∨).

The determinant restricts to the universal pencil as a universal binary (𝑛 + 1) -ic. The question that we answer in this note is: what is the class of the locus where this binary form has a repeated root? To this end, our main result is the following.
