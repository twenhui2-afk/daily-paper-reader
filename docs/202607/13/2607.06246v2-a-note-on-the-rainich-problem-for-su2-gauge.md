## 摘要
We provide resolution to non-Abelian Rainich problem。

核心做法：By canonically identifying traceless symmetric (0,2)-tensors 结合 Hermitian forms on 向量 bundle 的 chira。

主要结果：We then prove that existence 的 local \operatorname{SU}(2) Yang-Mills field 结合 prescribed stress-ener。

值得关注的点在于：这篇文章的价值在于把《用于SU (2) Gauge的Note on Rainich Problem》这个问题讲清楚。

## Abstract
We provide a resolution to the non-Abelian Rainich problem. By canonically identifying traceless symmetric $(0,2)$-tensors with Hermitian forms on the vector bundle of chiral 2-forms, we define the internal square roots of a stress-energy tensor. We then prove that the existence of a local $\operatorname{SU}(2)$ Yang-Mills field with prescribed stress-energy tensor $T$ is equivalent to a single differential condition on internal square roots of $T$.
