## 摘要
We address question 的 how small non-vanishing sum 的 N-th roots 的 unity 结合 k terms can be。

核心做法：We show upper bounds 的 shape N^{-α_k}，where α_karrow\infty 结合 k。

主要结果：We also address question 的 improving these bounds 用于 positive proportion 的 N。

值得关注的点在于：这篇文章的价值在于把《unity的roots的Small sums》这个问题讲清楚。

## Abstract
We address the question of how small a non-vanishing sum of $N$-th roots of unity with $k$ terms can be. We show upper bounds of the shape $N^{-α_k}$, where $α_k\rightarrow\infty$ with $k$. We also address the question of improving these bounds for a positive proportion of $N$.
