# A decomposition of Weyl group multiple Dirichlet series for symmetrizable Kac-Moody root systems 

Alexandru A. Popa, Jack Walsh 14 th July, 2026 

Abstract 

We study twisted Weyl group multiple Dirichlet series attached to symmetrizable Kac-Moody root systems, using the Chinta-Gunnells method to construct their p-parts. Our main result is a decomposition theorem for functions invariant under the twisted Chinta-Gunnells action: under natural analytic hypotheses, such a function has a unique expansion in terms of shifted Chinta-Gunnells averages, indexed by the dominant weights in the highest weight module determined by the twisting parameter. In particular, we show that this decomposition holds for twisted multiple Dirichlet series over rational function fields. For finite root systems, these results were proved by Friedlander [Fri23]. We also show that the relevant Chinta-Gunnells averages admit analytic continuation to the interior of the complexified Tits cone. In the affine eA1 case, we prove extra functional equations, not arising from the Weyl group, for the untwisted average and for averages twisted by fundamental weights. As a consequence, we obtain an explicit formula for the multiple Dirichlet series with square-free twisting parameters, and show that it also satisfies an extra functional equation. 

# 1 Introduction 

A Weyl group multiple Dirichlet series (WMDS) associated with a finite root system Φ of rank 

r is a Dirichlet series in r complex variables over a global field, satisfying a group of functional equations isomorphic to the Weyl group of Φ. They were first considered by Brubaker, Bump, Chinta, Friedberg, and Hoffstein in a series of papers [BBC +06, BBF06, BBFH07, BBF11b], in order to unify many objects that had been studied previously in a case-by-case basis. According to the Eisenstein conjecture , proposed in [BBC +06], they naturally appear as Whittaker coeffi-cients of an Eisenstein series on a global metaplectic group with root system Φ. For finite root systems, the Eisenstein conjecture was recently proved by Chen [Che24], after being proved in type A by Brubaker, Bump, and Friedberg [BBF11a], and in type B by Friedberg and Zhang [FZ15]. Historically, one of the main reasons for introducing such series was to extract arithmetic infor-mation about families of automorphic L-functions, such as nonvanishing of central values or the asymptotics of moments of quadratic twists of automorphic L-functions [GH85, BFH90]. A cen-tral example is provided by the WMDS associated with moments of the the family of quadratic Dirichlet L-functions. In this setting, the analytic properties of the series–its meromorphic cotinuation and the residues at its poles–govern the corresponding moment asymptotics. For a survey of what is known about moments of quadratic Dirichlet L-functions, we refer to the introduction of [DPP25].The WMDS associated with the r-th moment in this family is attached to a root system Φ r of rank r + 1, whose Dynkin diagram is star-shaped with a central node 1arXiv:2607.11834v1 [math.NT] 13 Jul 2026 connected to r exterior nodes. This root system is finite dimensional for r ≤ 3, affine for r = 4, hyperbolic Kac-Moody if r = 5, and indefinite Kac-Moody for r ≥ 6. In this paper we are concerned with twisted WMDS for symmetrizable Kac-Moody root systems, which were first studied in [LZ15]. We now describe their construction, specializing to the function field setting considered in this paper. Finally, we mention a different construction of a canonical untwisted MDS over the rational func-tion field for arbitrary Φ, using a set of axioms that includes the local-to-global principle. The axiomatic approach was initiated by Diaconu and Pa¸ sol for star-shaped root systems in [DP18], further developed by Whitehead for root systems of type eAr [Whi16], and later generalised by Sawin [Saw24] to a setting that includes the WMDS associated with symmetrizable Kac-Moody root systems. The axiomatic approach departs from the traditional one, in that the WMDS is not assumed to satisfy a group of functional equations. However, Sawin and Whitehead showed that the resulting WMDS satisfies functional equations that are equivalent to invariance under the untwisted Chinta-Gunnells action in the symmetrizable Kac-Moody case [SW25]. There-fore, the decomposition (2) makes sense for the axiomatic MDS, and it is an interesting question to investigate the coefficients appearing in this decomposition. In the eD4 case it was shown in [DIPP25] that the WMDS constructed with normalized π-part eZ0 above agrees with the ax-iomatic MDS. For star-shaped root systems Φ r with r > 4, relating the axiomatic MDS to the one constructed from π-parts is an interesting open problem, in view of its applications to the asymptotics of r-th moments of Dirichlet L-functions.

6.5 Extra functional equation satisfied by twisted MDS 

We now show that the multiple Dirichlet series Z(s; c) for eA1 also satisfies an extra functional equation of the type satisfied by its π-parts in §6.2 and §6.3. We assume, as in the previous section, that c0 and c1 are coprime, monic, square-free polynomials. In the untwisted case, Corollary 6.7 already shows that Z(s) is invariant under the action |0τ

defined in §6.2. For general c, the situation is complicated by the fact that the coefficients of 

Z(s; c) as a power series in x = q−s are no longer polynomials in u. Indeed, for deg c ≥ 3, the Dirichlet polynomials L(s, χ c) in Theorem 6.5 have coefficients which are not polynomial in q.Instead, we use the fact that these coefficients are q-Weil numbers, as we now recall. Let C = Cd be the hyperelliptic curve y2 = d(t) for d ∈ Fq[t] square-free. Its zeta function is 

ZC (t) = PC (t)(1 − t)(1 − qt )with PC (t) ∈ Z[t] of degree 2 g, where g the genus of C, and constant term PC (0) = 1.
