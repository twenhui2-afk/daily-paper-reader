ON q-PRE-LIE ALGEBRAS 

CHENGYANG LU AND YANYONG HONG (CORRESPONDING AUTHOR) Abstract . In this paper, we introduce the notion of q-pre-Lie algebras from the perspective of representations of Lie algebras, providing a parametrized generalization that unifies pre-Lie al-gebras and anti-pre-Lie algebras. For a q-pre-Lie algebra ( A, ◦), the commutator of ◦ is a Lie bracket and the left multiplication operator scaled by q gives a representation of the associated commutator Lie algebra. We also introduce the notions of q-O-operators and q-Novikov alge-bras, and investigate their relationships with q-pre-Lie algebras. Several explicit constructions of q-pre-Lie algebras are provided. Moreover, we give a complete classification of graded q-pre-Lie algebra structures on the Witt algebra and prove the nonexistence of such structures on the Virasoro algebra when q , 1. Finally, for finite-dimensional complex simple Lie algebras, we show that compatible root-graded q-pre-Lie algebras exist on sl 2(C) precisely when q = 2 or 

q = −1, and do not exist on any other simple Lie algebra.

The notion of pre-Lie algebras, also known as left-symmetric algebras or Vinberg algebras, arose from the study of a ffi ne structures on Lie groups [16], convex homogeneous cones [21] and the deformation theory of associative algebras [11], and has since appeared in many fields of mathematics and physics, such as vertex algebras [4, 5]. Recall that a pre-Lie algebra is a pair ( A, ◦), where A is a vector space equipped with a binary operation satisfying (a ◦ b) ◦ c − a ◦ (b ◦ c) = (b ◦ a) ◦ c − b ◦ (a ◦ c), a, b, c ∈ A.

A key property of pre-Lie algebras is that the commutator [ a, b] : = a ◦ b − b ◦ a defines a Lie algebra structure on A, and the left multiplication operator L◦ gives a representation of the associated commutator Lie algebra. In recent years, the study of variants and generalizations of pre-Lie algebras has attracted increasing attention. A particularly notable variant is that of anti-pre-Lie algebras [19], which are binary operations ◦ such that the commutator [ ·, ·] is a Lie bracket and the negative left mul-tiplication operator −L◦ is a representation of the associated Lie algebra. This representation-theoretic characterization of anti-pre-Lie algebras stands in direct parallel to the classical prop-erty of pre-Lie algebras, where L◦ itself is a representation. Motivated by this parallel, it is natural to seek a unified framework that interpolates between these two structures. Following this idea, we introduce the notion of q-pre-Lie algebras (see Definition 2.1): a bi-nary operation ◦ such that the commutator [ ·, ·] is a Lie bracket and the scaled left multiplication operator qL ◦ is a representation of the associated Lie algebra. This parametrized framework uni-fies pre-Lie algebras (the case q = 1) and anti-pre-Lie algebras (the case q = −1), and provides a systematic approach to studying Lie-admissible algebras with scaled representations. Our first main result establishes a fundamental characterization: for q , 0, ( A, ◦) is a q-pre-Lie algebra if and only if ( A, [·, ·]) is a Lie algebra and ( A, qL ◦) is a representation of it. We further provide several explicit constructions of q-pre-Lie algebras, including those arising from symmetric bilinear forms and from derivations and q-derivations of commutative associative algebras. We then introduce the notion of q-O-operators on Lie algebras, which generalizes the classical O-operators. A key result is that a q-O-operator induces a q-pre-Lie algebra structure on the representation space if and only if it is strong. Finally, we investigate compatible root-graded q-pre-Lie algebra structures on finite-dimensional complex simple Lie algebras. To-gether with earlier results for anti-pre-Lie algebras (the case q = −1) obtained in [2], we show that sl 2(C) is the only finite-dimensional complex simple Lie algebra admitting a compatible root-graded q-pre-Lie algebra structure for q = 2 or q = −1.
