## Abstract
In this paper, we establish asymptotic formulas for the first and second twisted moments of $r$-th order Hecke $L$-functions over global fields that contain the $2r$-th roots of unity, for $r\ge 3$. We focus primarily on algebraic number fields. As a consequence, we establish a positive proportion of non-vanishing central values for these $L$-functions, specifically for families of both square-free and $r$-th power-free ideals. Our approach is based on the machinery of multiple Dirichlet series.
