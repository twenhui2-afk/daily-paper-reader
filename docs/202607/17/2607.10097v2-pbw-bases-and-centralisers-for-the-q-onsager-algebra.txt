# PBW bases and centralisers for the q-Onsager algebra 

Haoran Zhu ∗

Abstract 

We prove that, over an arbitrary field and whenever q is not a root of unity, the Baseilhac– Kolb root vectors form a PBW basis of the q-Onsager algebra for every total order on the positive roots of bsl 2. This removes the previous transcendence hypothesis. We establish twelve PBW bases in the alternating generators and show that they persist under arbitrary scalar central specialisation of the alternating central extension. We determine the centraliser of the negative alternating subalgebra and that of the first imaginary alternating generator, and deduce that the four single-family alternating polynomial subalgebras are maximal commutative. Together, these results settle four conjectures of Terwilliger and, in characteristic different from 2, a conjecture of Baseilhac and Belliard. 

Keywords: q-Onsager algebra, PBW basis, alternating generators, root vectors, central specialisa-tion, centraliser. 

Mathematics Subject Classification: 17B37, 17B67, 81R50. 

# 1 Introduction 

The Onsager algebra first appeared in Onsager’s solution of the two-dimensional Ising model [25]. Its two-generator presentation by the Dolan–Grady relations provides an algebraic construction of the commuting quantities that occur in Onsager-type integrability [18]. The q-Onsager algebra Oq

is the corresponding q-deformation. It appears in boundary quantum integrable systems [27, 4, 10, 5, 6], in the theory of tridiagonal algebras [28], and as a basic example of a quantum symmetric-pair coideal subalgebra [22, 20, 24, 23]. There are two principal PBW constructions for Oq. Baseilhac and Kolb used braid-group automorphisms to define real and imaginary root vectors, modelled on Damiani’s root vectors for U + 

> q

(bsl 2), and obtained a root-vector PBW basis [15, 11]. A second construction comes from the current algebra associated with the reflection equation [10, 12, 8]. Terwilliger identified this current algebra with the alternating central extension Aq of Oq and proved the factorisation Aq ≃

Oq ⊗F[z1, z 2, . . . ] [33, 34, 35]. The four current families descend under the canonical central reduction to the alternating generators of Oq. We write 

W − = {W−n | n ⩾ 0}, W + = {Wn+1 | n ⩾ 0},G = {Gn+1 | n ⩾ 0}, eG = { eGn+1 | n ⩾ 0}.

They then conjectured that the ordered monomials in the three blocks W − < G < W + form a PBW basis [7, Conjec-ture 1]; they called this the W G -basis . The conjecture gives more than generation by W0, W 1: it gives a unique normal form in the current coefficients after the central quantum determinant has been assigned scalar values. Terwilliger’s factorisation of Aq identifies every scalar central fibre with Oq, but it does not by itself prove the W G -basis conjecture. A central specialisation changes each alternating generator by lower-index terms, and a four-family PBW basis of Aq does not formally give a three-family PBW basis in a quotient. Terwilliger therefore proposed six three-family orders for W −, eG, W + [34, Conjecture 16.2]. He also asked whether the negative and imaginary alternating subalgebras are self-centralising [34, Conjectures 16.7 and 16.8]. More recently, the author proved the four-family PBW order W− < G < eG < W+ for Aq [36]. The present paper treats the corresponding three-family bases after central specialisation, together with the root-vector and centraliser problems for 

Oq.The order in a PBW basis is part of the structure. Factorised universal R-matrices are asso-ciated with specified convex orders [19, 14, 16], and the same order is visible in Gauss or LDU decompositions of L-operators [17]. In the coideal setting, universal and tensor K-matrices provide the corresponding structures [3, 1, 2]. For the q-Onsager algebra, boundary and fused K-operators occur in closely related constructions [13, 21]. Alternating normal orders also enter recent calcula-tions of universal TT- and TQ-relations for Aq [9]. A PBW theorem in every scalar central fibre therefore provides a normal form for these current coefficients after the central parameters have been fixed.
