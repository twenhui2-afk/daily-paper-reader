1. INTRODUCTION 

The projective representations of the symmetric groups Sn are rep-resentations of a central extension ˜Sn of the symmetric group by a cyclic group of order two. When the generator z ∈ Z( ˜Sn) satisfies 

ϕ(z) = 1 , the representation is called ordinary and for ϕ(z) = −1 we get a spin representation. The p-strict partitions labeling spin represen-tations represent the irreducible modules over a field of characteristic 

p [MY1,MY2]. Leclerc and Thibon [LT] conjectured that the spin rep-resentation for affine Lie algebra corresponds to the simple modules of highest weight representation V (Λ) for the case Λ = Λ 0, and it was proven in [BK]. In our last paper [AS1] we conjecture an algorithm to construct spin multipartitions for general highest weight modules V (Λ) 

in A(2) 2n , where h = 2 n + 1 .In this paper we construct partitions for the case Λ = Λ n, and get partitions of special form. Also we get some result for the canonical basis elements and we define a slice in the crystal corresponding to this case. For a Kac-Moody algebra g of classical affine type one can construct a q-deformed Fock space of level 1, and in [AS1] it was shown that the Fock space is a module for the quantum group Uq(g) in the case where g is of type A(2) 2n . Leclerc and Thibon [LT] studied this Fock space for Λ = Λ 0 and get a connection with spin representations of Sm in characteristic h, where h is prime, such that the action of the standard

Canonical basis elements lie in Fock spaces, which are modules over the quantum enveloping algebras. For a type A, a good reference for this material which does not require using infinite wedge products is the work of Lascoux, Leclerc and Thibon, [§4,[LLT]].
