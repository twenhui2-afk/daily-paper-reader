Let η = ( η1, . . . , η n) ∈ { +, −} n be nonconstant, and read subscripts mod-ulo n. The circular fence Cη is the transitive closure of 

ηi = + = ⇒ xi ≻ xi+1 , ηi = − =⇒ xi ≺ xi+1 .

Nonconstancy makes this orientation acyclic and ensures that both signs occur. Thus Cη is a poset, and its order-preserving maps to [m] are the cyclic sequences satisfying the corresponding weak inequalities. Rank-matrix and trace models for related oriented and circular fences were developed by Kantarcı Oğuz [10]. If one sign occurs only once, its edge is redundant in the transitive closure and the Hasse diagram is a path. The record identity below includes this case; the comparison with Kahane’s circular blocks is restricted to orientations whose Hasse diagram is a cycle. 

Proposition 4.1 (Trace formula) . For a positive integer m, let 

M+[a, b ] = 1a≥b, M−[a, b ] = 1a≤b (a, b ∈ [m]) .

Then 

Ω( Cη; m) = tr( Mη1 · · · Mηn ).

Proof. The (a1, a n+1 )-entry of the product sums the indicators of the edge inequalities over a2, . . . , a n ∈ [m]. Taking the trace imposes an+1 = a1, and hence counts the order-preserving maps. This is the standard transfer-matrix argument; compare [18, §4.7] and [10, Proposition 5.3]. □

Lemma 4.3 (Closing a greedy block path) . Let C be a circular fence whose Hasse diagram is a cycle, and let ys be the ascent vertex with largest label among all ascent vertices. Delete the incoming cover ys−1 < y s, and read the resulting path from ys to ys−1. If B1, . . . , B k is Kahane’s greedy block partition of this path, then the same consecutive blocks form the unique valid circular block partition of C.Proof. The first block has root ys. Every later ascent root has smaller label by the choice of ys, while a descent root is created by a downward threshold reset and therefore also has smaller label. If k = 1 , all other ascent labels lie below the label of ys, and every descent label lies above it; this is the whole-cycle block condition. Suppose k ≥ 2. The path blocks are consecutive intervals. Restoring the deleted cover joins the last interval to the first and changes no inequality inside a block. At the block level the new relation runs from Bk to B1, and the root of Bk has smaller label than the root ys of B1. Hence the restored partition satisfies Kahane’s circular validity condition. Uniqueness follows from [9, Lemma 4.7]. □

8. Record caterpillars and terminal values 

Atkinson’s algorithm computes the spectrum of a distinguished element in a tree poset in quadratic time [1]. Record trees have caterpillar cover graphs, so that recursion takes the following explicit form in record-gap coordinates. By Theorem 7.5, the distinguished-element spectrum is the terminal-value distribution in a fixed record-set fiber. 

Lemma 8.1 (Record-caterpillar structure) . Write R = {r1 < · · · < r s = n}
