## 摘要
We prove Miller's conjectured bound 用于 solvable groups：if G is finite solvable group 与 χ\in\Irr (G)。

核心做法：We also prove same bound 用于 monomial irreducible characters 的 arbitrary finite groups。

主要结果：摘要给出了正向结果。

值得关注的点在于：这篇文章的价值在于把《用于Solvable Groups的Characters的Unity的Zeros与Roots》这个问题讲清楚。

## Abstract
We prove Miller's conjectured bound for solvable groups: if $G$ is a finite solvable group and $χ\in\Irr(G)$, then $χ(g)$ is zero or a root of unity for at least half of the elements $g\in G$. We also prove the same bound for monomial irreducible characters of arbitrary finite groups.
