JUNYU LU 

Abstract. Let Kt = Q(θt), where θt is the largest root of X3 − tX 2 − ( t + 3)X − 1and t ⩾ −1 is an integer. We classify the points in En(Kt) with abscissa θt − 1, for 

En : y2 = x3 −n2x, when n is a positive integer and En(Q) has rank zero. The main step excludes every nonzero two-torsion value of the group trace. The classification reduces to v2 = 2n2 − 9, and the conjugates of every resulting point generate a subgroup of rank two. A classical quartic equation then gives exactly four pairs (d, t) with d > 0rational for which (θt − 1)/d 2 is an abscissa on E3. Without a rank assumption, we exclude the abscissa θt − 1 on E5(Kt) and E6(Kt) and prove that only finitely many parameters t admit this abscissa for each fixed positive integer n. For an integral shift 

θt − r, we obtain a simultaneous-square criterion for trace zero. We use it to construct points on E3 over infinitely many pairwise nonisomorphic simplest cubic fields.
