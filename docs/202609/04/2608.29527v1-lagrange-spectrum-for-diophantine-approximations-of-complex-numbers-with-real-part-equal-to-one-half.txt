LAGRANGE SPECTRUM FOR DIOPHANTINE APPROXIMATIONS OF COMPLEX NUMBERS WITH REAL PART EQUAL TO ONE HALF 

HAO CHENG, HAROLD ERAZO, CARLOS GUSTAVO MOREIRA, AND THIAGO VASCONCELOS 

Abstract. Motivated by a theorem of A. Schmidt on the part of the complex Lagrange spectrum below 2, we study the restricted Lagrange spectrum L 12 +iR arising from the approximation of com-plex numbers of the form 12 + iα , α ∈ R \ Q by Gaussian rationals 

p/q with p, q ∈ Z[i], q̸ = 0. We show that this spectrum ad-mits a description in terms of a dynamical spectrum associated with a real horseshoe. As a consequence, we obtain several fractal properties of L 12 +iR, such as continuity of the dimension function 

t 7 → dim H (L 12 +iR ∩ (−∞ , t )). We also prove that the set of complex numbers z satisfying 

z − pq ≥ 12|q|2 , for all p, q ∈ Z[i], q ̸ = 0 ,

is uncountable. In fact, we show that this inequality holds for every complex number of the form z = 12 (1 + iθ ) where θ ∈ R \ Q is a root of one of Schmidt’s C-minimal forms.
