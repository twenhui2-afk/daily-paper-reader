Abstract 

Let C = ( f ) be a λ-constacyclic code over Fq , where f (X) is a monic factor of XN − λ with nonzero constant term. We introduce the support period sp( f ) of f (X), and define its support-primitive core fsp (X) as the unique support-primitive polynomial satisfying f (X) = fsp (Xs), where s = sp( f ). We show that this polynomial relation induces a Hamming-weight-preserving linear isomorphism C ∼= C s

> sp

, where Csp is the support-primitive core of C, and prove that this decomposition is intrinsic to the code. We give two equivalent descriptions of the support period: a coefficient-based one and a roots-based one. In the repeated-root case, the latter is determined by the p-adic structure and the stabilizer of the defining function, while in the simple-root case it is determined by the coarsest multiple equal-difference representation of the defining set. We then derive coding-theoretic consequences for the Hamming distance, weight enumerator, covering radius, and Euclidean duality. In particular, the arithmetic Singleton bound of a simple-root constacyclic code is identified with the classical Singleton bound of its support-primitive core. Finally, we apply the decomposition to cyclic codes with reducible generator polynomials and obtain bounds for their arithmetic Singleton values. 

KeyWords. Constacyclic codes; support-primitive decomposition; support period; finite fields.

Thus, in addition to its linear structure, a constacyclic code carries a natural arithmetic structure coming from its generator polynomial. In recent works, the authors have investigated the Hamming distance of constacyclic codes through this arithmetic structure. A basic theme in this study is to understand the symmetry of the roots of a generator polynomial and then translate it into information about the polynomial itself and the code. In [5], the multiple equal-difference (MED) structure of cyclotomic cosets was introduced to describe an additive symmetry of their elements. In [6], this structure was related to the coefficient structure of polynomials: equal-difference root sets correspond to binomial polynomials, and the resulting root–coefficient correspondence was used to study minimal binomial multiples of polynomials and, in particular, Hamming distance 2 constacyclic codes. In [7], the MED structure was generalized from cyclotomic cosets to the defining sets of squarefree polynomials and was used to obtain a family of upper bounds on the Hamming distance of simple-root constacyclic codes, including the arithmetic Singleton bound. The purpose of the present paper is to further develop this coefficient-side and root-side viewpoint. We introduce invariants that describe the relevant symmetry of a generator polynomial from both sides, and prove that these descriptions are equivalent. On the root side, the corresponding information is expressed in terms of the p-adic structure of the defining function and its stabilizer. Let N be a positive integer. A linear code C of length N over Fq is a linear subspace of FNq . The dimension of C is defined to be its dimension as a Fq-space. Following this fact, we will refer to the number of nonzero coefficients of a polynomial f (X) ∈ Fq[X] as the Hamming weight of f (X), and denote it by wt H(f ). According to whether XN − λ has repeated roots or not, a λ-constacyclic code of length N is said to be repeated-root if p | N , while is said to be simple-root if p ∤ N .
