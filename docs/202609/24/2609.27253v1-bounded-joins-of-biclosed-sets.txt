BOUNDED JOINS OF BICLOSED SETS        

> YIBO GAO, YULIN PENG, AND HANLIN XU
> Abstract. Dyer conjectured that the join of two biclosed sets of positive roots can be described by increasing Bruhat paths whose reflection labels belong to their union. We give a type-uniform proof of this conjecture for all finite Coxeter groups. More generally, for a 2-coclosed set Cof positive roots contained in an inversion set, we show that its 2-closure is an inversion set I(w) and that the elements reachable from the identity using reflections labeled by roots in Cform exactly the set [ e, w ]Bw−1. The proof combines Dyer’s closure criteria with a root-selection argument.

1. Introduction 

Let ( W, S ) be a Coxeter system with its standard real root system Φ = Φ + ⊔ − Φ+. For 

w ∈ W , write I(w) = {α ∈ Φ+ : w−1α ∈ − Φ+} for its (left) inversion set . The right and left weak orders , denoted by ≤R and ≤L, respectively, are defined by prefixes and suffixes of reduced expressions: u ≤R w if some reduced expression for u is a prefix of a reduced expression for w, and u ≤L w if some reduced expression for u is a suffix of a reduced expression for w.It is classical that u ≤R w if and only if I(u) ⊆ I(w), and that the weak order of a finite Coxeter group is a lattice. More generally, for an arbitrary Coxeter system, weak order is a complete meet-semilattice [6, Theorem 3.2.1]. For infinite Coxeter groups, the meet always exists, while the existence of a join requires an upper bound. The description of weak order by containment of inversion sets suggests describing joins in terms of roots. A subset of Φ + is 2 -closed if it contains every positive root in the nonnegative span of any two of its members. Its 2 -closure is denoted by cl 2. A set is 2 -coclosed if its complement in Φ + is 2-closed, and 2 -biclosed if both it and its complement are 2-closed. Dyer proved that whenever u ∨R v exists, its inversion set is given by I(u ∨R v) = cl 2(I(u) ∪ I(v)), and the same description holds for arbitrary set of elements of W with a common upper bound [8, Theorem 1.5]. Let B(Φ +) be the inclusion poset of 2-biclosed subsets of Φ +, called the extended weak order . Its finite elements are precisely the inversion sets, so it contains the weak order on 

W as an order ideal and coincides with it when W is finite [8, §2.3]. Dyer conjectured that 

B(Φ +) is always a complete lattice, with joins given by the 2-closure of unions [8, §2.5]. Barkley and Speyer classified biclosed sets in affine root systems and established the lattice property in types ˜A and ˜C [2]. They subsequently proved the complete lattice property and the closure formula for joins in all affine types [3]. Beyond affine type, Barkley, Defant, Hersh, McCammond, McConville, and Speyer proved these assertions for the rank-three universal Coxeter group [1]. The conjecture studied here asks for a further description of these joins through increasing paths in the Bruhat graph.

Biagioli and Perrone [5] proved the conjecture for finite dihedral groups and groups of type A, and verified it computationally in types H3 and F4. Dermenjian [7] showed that inversion sets, and more generally initial sections of reflection orders, are fixed by Bruhat preclosure. He also proved that iterated Bruhat preclosure yields the inversion set of a bounded weak join in arbitrary Coxeter groups, and established idempotence on arbitrary reflection subsets in type A, giving another proof in that type. His examples show that idempotence fails for arbitrary reflection subsets in other types.
