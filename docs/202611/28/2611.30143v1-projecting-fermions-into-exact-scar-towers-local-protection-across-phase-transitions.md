We construct exact scar towers in SO (n) (n\ge3) spin chains by Gutzwiller 投影的 n-flavor flat-band Ki。

核心做法：projected roots have only singlet 与 antisymmetric components on each bond。

## Abstract
We construct exact scar towers in $SO(n)$ ($n\ge3$) spin chains by Gutzwiller projection of $n$-flavor flat-band Kitaev chains. The projected roots have only singlet and antisymmetric components on each bond. These local constraints allow a staggered flavor flip to generate an equidistant tower. The root and its first descendant exhaust the two-site configurations that are visited by the tower, leaving an orthogonal bond subspace unvisited. Deformations within this subspace can break $SO(n)$ symmetry and reshape the low-energy physics without altering the exact scar tower. A polarization-selective deformation drives an exact first-order transition from the symmetry-protected topological phase to a ferromagnetic phase. The tower retains its wave functions and absolute energies across the phase transition, including at the gapless point. Exact scars therefore need not inherit the symmetries, order parameters, or criticality of the ground state.
