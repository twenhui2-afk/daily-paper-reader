## Abstract
Analytic calculations up to single-scale quantities in perturbative quantum field theory result into special numbers and functions, which are generalizations as iterative integrals over special alphabets. In this way one obtains polylogarithms, Nielsen integrals, the iterated integrals over linear denominator terms, cyclotomic letters, letters induced by quadratic forms, and square-root valued letters. The inverse Mellin transform relates special nested sums to these quantities, which are harmonic sums, generalized harmonic sums, nested sums implied by quadratic forms, cyclotomic harmonic sums, and nested sums containing central binomials. We construct the $μ$- and $q$-extensions of all these special functions. They are expected to occur in loop calculations within $μ$- and $q$-extended quantum field theories via modified (anti)commutation relations. The $μ$-extended special functions are quite different form the corresponding $q$-extended functions. While in the former case one observes class-preservation characterized by the polynomial $μ$-parameter, except for the case of root-valued letters. In the latter case new higher transcendental functions are obtained.
