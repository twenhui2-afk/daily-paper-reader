## Abstract
Let $X$ be a connected finite-type CW-complex with a map to the circle that is surjective on fundamental groups. If $X$ is formal, the algebraic monodromy of the associated infinite cyclic cover has no Jordan block of size greater than one at the eigenvalue 1, over the rationals; formality of the $d$-fold cyclic cover rules out such blocks at the $d$-th roots of unity. We study how formality of finite abelian covers and Jordan blocks of the monodromy constrain each other. We call $X$ Alexander formal if there are no such blocks at any root of unity, rationally, nor at the eigenvalue 1 modulo any prime; this is a formality property of cochains, as DG modules over the cohomology of the circle. The complement of a central hyperplane arrangement, with its Milnor fibration, is Alexander formal exactly when the numbers of hyperplanes in its irreducible factors have no common divisor greater than 1. We also show that a CDGA graded by an abelian group, over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, is formal if and only if it is formal as a graded CDGA. Hence the strong formality of Kohno and Pajitnov implies formality of every finite abelian cover of $X$, but not conversely. Modulo $p$, eigenvalues of $p$-power order collide with 1, producing Jordan blocks that strong formality allows; the two conditions are incomparable, and all four resulting classes contain arrangement complements.
