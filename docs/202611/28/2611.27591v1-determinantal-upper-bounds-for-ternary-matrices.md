## Abstract
We obtain a new, sharper upper bound for the determinant of a matrix of order $n \equiv 2 \pmod{3}$ with entries in the complex third-roots of unity. This bound is an analog of Ehlich's bound for binary matrices of order $n \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$ and it is obtained by first applying an appropriate normalization, or balancing, procedure for matrices with root-of-unity entries and then generalizing Ehlich's argument. This new bound achieves an asymptotic fraction of approximately 74% of the Hadamard bound.
