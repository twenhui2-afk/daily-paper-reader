## Abstract
We study Frobenius polynomials attached to linearly constrained Kloosterman families, taking the rank-$r(n)=2^n-\binom{n}{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$, weight-$(n+1)$ sheaf of the author's companion papers as an explicit input. The present contribution is the passage from that sheaf to local characteristic polynomials and the intermediate-extension intersection cohomology on $(\mathbb P^1)^n$. We prove the local reciprocal-root bounds and the degreewise purity of the completed factors; their weights are $n+1+i$ in cohomological degree $i$. Using the imported arithmetic relative-image identification, we construct a reflection pairing and derive the completed functional equation with positive root number. We also give a canonical Frobenius-equivariant splitting of the fibrewise compact-support/parabolic sequence by separation of weights and examine the comparison on open coordinate-zero divisors. The statements do not assume global arithmetic Frobenius semisimplicity or a higher-codimension wild-boundary exchange. An explicit source-and-dependency ledger distinguishes results imported from the companion papers from deductions established here.
