## Abstract
A tree $T$ on $q$ edges is said to be $0$-rotatable if for every vertex $v \in V(T)$, there exists a graceful labeling $f : V(T) \to \{0, 1, \dots, q\}$ such that $f(v) = 0$. Rofa proved that all regular (symmetric) spider trees with leg length at most $3$ are $0$-rotatable and conjectured that the property holds for every leg length. In this note, we settle the first open case of this conjecture by proving that every regular spider tree $T(k, 4)$ with $k \ge 1$ legs of length $4$ is $0$-rotatable. Our proof combines Rofa's algebraic graceful labeling at the root with complementary labelings, an end-to-end leg-reversal construction placing $0$ at a leaf, and a two-leg reflection permutation placing $0$ at distance $2$ from the root.
