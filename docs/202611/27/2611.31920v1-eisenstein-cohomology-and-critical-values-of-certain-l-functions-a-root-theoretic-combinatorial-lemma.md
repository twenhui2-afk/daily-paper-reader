## Abstract
This paper gives a root-theoretic extension of the Harder--Raghuram combinatorial lemma, relating criticality of adjacent $L-$values to degrees in nilradical cohomology, to maximal parabolics $P=MU$ of connected split reductive groups with irreducible, essentially tempered cohomological inducing data.   At real places, the paper classifies the maximal parabolics for which the two Kostant degrees in the boundary comparison can agree at a regular shift. For each such parabolic, an admissible shift makes both adjacent arguments critical in every Langlands--Shahidi grade exactly when both Kostant degrees equal $\dim U/2$. The classification recovers the two-block parabolics of type $A$ with even product of block sizes (Harder--Raghuram, proved by Weselmann) and the isotropic-line parabolic of type $D_{2m+1}$ for $m\ge2$ (Bhagwat--Raghuram). The only exceptional cases are $P_1$ and $P_6$ of type $E_6$. In all other cases, roots of $U$ fixed by the longest Levi Weyl element confine simultaneous criticality to a singular wall, generalizing the odd-block obstruction for $\mathrm{GL}_N$. Their number is the difference of the fundamental ranks of $M$ and $G$. When the Kostant degrees differ, as in Harder's $\mathrm{GL}_2$ setting, the paper determines which boundary degrees can still coincide.   At a complex place, an admissible shift is simultaneously critical exactly when the two conjugate Levi weights after the shift occur in Kostant degrees summing to $\dim U$. With a common relative center, total degree balance at a regular admissible shift characterizes simultaneous criticality at every archimedean place. For split $E_6$ over $\mathbb Q$, a half-spin family yields Eisenstein cohomology classes in degrees $20$ and $21$, including a degree-$20$ class with nonzero restrictions to both associate boundary face types.
