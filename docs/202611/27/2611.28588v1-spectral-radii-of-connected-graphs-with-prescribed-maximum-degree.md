## Abstract
For a real number $α>0$ and a positive integer $r$, we give recursive characterizations of the existence of finite simple connected graphs $G$ with adjacency spectral radius $ρ(G)=α$ and maximum degree $Δ(G)\le r$ (or $=r$). Adding one vertex to a graph $F$ at a time to update the Schur-complement $q=α-{\mathbf b}^{\mathsf T}B^{-1}{\mathbf b}$, where $B=αI-A(F)$ is positive definite and ${\mathbf b}$ is a nonzero $0$--$1$ that specifies the neighbours of $z$ in $F$. Realizability is characterized by admissible constructions with positive intermediate outputs $q$ and a final zero output. We also establish a reduction for a graph $G$ obtained by attaching pairwise disjoint rooted trees, each by a single edge, to a nonempty connected graph $H$. Assume that $αI-A(T)\succ0$ for every attached tree $T$. For each $z\in V(H)$, let $q_{z,1},\ldots,q_{z,s_z}$ be the positive root pivots obtained by the scalar tree recursion for the trees attached to $z$, and set   \[   a_z=α-\sum_{j=1}^{s_z}q_{z,j}^{-1},   \qquad   M_H(\mathbf a)=\operatorname{diag}(a_z:z\in V(H))-A(H),   \] where an empty sum is zero. Then $ρ(G)=α$ if and only if $M_H(\mathbf a)\succeq0$ and $\det M_H(\mathbf a)=0$. Taking $H=K_1$ recovers the tree criteria, while $δ(H)\geq2$ gives criteria for graphs with prescribed 2-core $H$. In particular, for unicyclic graphs of prescribed cycle length, path continuants reduce the spectral condition to positivity inequalities and one scalar equation.
