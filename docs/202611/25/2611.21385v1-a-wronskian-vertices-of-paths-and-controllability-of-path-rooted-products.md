## Abstract
For a graph \(G\) with adjacency and degree matrices \(A(G)\) and \(D(G)\), set \(A_α(G)=αD(G)+(1-α)A(G)\) for \(0\leqα<1\). Using Chebyshev polynomials and a theorem of Conway and Jones, we classify the \(A_α\)-Wronskian vertices of every path \(P_n\), \(n\geq2\) (equivalently, the standard basis vectors cyclic for \(A_α(P_n)\)). The central vertex of an odd-order path is never Wronskian; for every other vertex, we determine an explicit finite set of algebraic exceptional parameters. These sets contain no rational parameters other than \(0\) and \(1/2\), for which the adjacency and signless Laplacian cases are characterized by gcd criteria. For even-order paths rooted at either central vertex, we explicitly evaluate the root resultant and the walk-determinant polynomial in the rooted-product factorization. The resulting controllability criterion combines \(A_α\)-controllability of the base graph with at most three explicit spectral exclusions. For each parameter, one of two fixed complementary base graphs yields controllable products for every even-order path and both central roots. For \(1/2\leqα<1\), algebraic conjugates further simplify the spectral test for general rooted products.
