## Abstract
Periapical radiographs (PRs) are central to endodontic diagnosis, but interpreting them and preparing reports are time-consuming. Technical language can limit patients' understanding, while existing automated methods often lack tooth-level links between findings and image evidence. We present PeriaTrace, a three-stage framework integrating tooth-level diagnosis, visually grounded report generation, and patient-friendly explanations and care recommendations. PeriaTrace was developed and evaluated using 12,877 PRs from an internal dataset and two external clinical cohorts. In internal evaluation, it achieved a macro Dice coefficient of 85.35%, BLEU-4 of 0.6504, an F1 score of 94.37% for joint tooth localization and Fédération Dentaire Internationale (FDI) identification, and a grounding-tag F1 score of 77.93%. In the two external cohorts, senior endodontists considered 91.16% and 84.52% of reports suitable for retention or revision without complete rewriting. Generation averaged 4.41 seconds per PR, several tens of times faster than manual drafting. Among 196 survey participants without medical backgrounds, 96.4% preferred reports with plain-language explanations and care recommendations, and 84.7% found visual localization cues helpful. PeriaTrace may enable faster, evidence-traceable reporting while supporting clinical review and patient communication. (Abridged version)
