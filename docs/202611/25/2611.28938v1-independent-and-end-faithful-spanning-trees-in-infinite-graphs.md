## Abstract
Spanning trees rooted at the same vertex are independent if, for each vertex other than the root, the paths from the root to that vertex are pairwise distinct and internally vertex-disjoint. Zehavi and Itai conjectured that, for every positive integer $k$, every finite $k$-connected graph has $k$ independent spanning trees rooted at any prescribed vertex. We show that every $2$-connected graph, finite or infinite, has two independent spanning trees rooted at any prescribed vertex. For locally finite $2$-connected graphs, both trees can be chosen end-faithful. We also prove that every locally finite $3$-connected chordal graph has three independent end-faithful spanning trees. Counterexamples show that local finiteness is essential for the version with independent end-faithful spanning trees. We further show that every $3$-connected graph with a universal vertex or a finite vertex cover has three independent spanning trees rooted at any prescribed vertex.
