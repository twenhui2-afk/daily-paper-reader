## Abstract
We extend the Segments Equal Sharing (SES) method of \citet{wu2024} from line highways to general highway networks, including networks with cycles. The input is an exogenously specified profile of route flows. We introduce the Weighted Proportional Sharing (WPS) method, which distributes each route's mileage revenue in proportion to weighted segment lengths and its fixed-surcharge revenue in proportion to positive segment surcharges. Route SES is recovered under homogeneous weights within each revenue component. We show that WPS is the sum of two weighted Shapley allocations and that the underlying route games extend the dividend and Shapley representation of linear SES. Two axiomatic characterizations identify WPS. The first uses additivity and proportional treatment of segments used on every positive-revenue route. The second combines sub-highway efficiency and weighted average balanced contributions under a route cancellation rule. We also extend Segments Proportional Sharing to general networks, identify it with the $τ$ value of the corresponding route game, and characterize it axiomatically. For Segments Compensated Sharing we characterize a weighted average tree allocation by consistency across rooted branch settlements and averaging under a specified root lottery. A cyclic example shows that different route splits can change the allocation even when origin--destination volumes and aggregate toll revenue are held fixed.
