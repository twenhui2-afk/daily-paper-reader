## Abstract
For a crystallographic root system $Φ$ with Weyl group $W$ and root lattice $L = \mathbb{Z}Φ$, we determine the minimum norm of a vector of $L$ having trivial stabilizer in $W$, and we prove that the vectors attaining it form a single orbit of $W$. Although this is a minimization of a quadratic function over the lattice points of a cone, the minimizer in the dominant chamber is always unique: it is $ρ+ λ$, where $ρ$ is the Weyl vector and $λ$ is $0$ or the minuscule weight in the class of $ρ$ modulo $L$. In particular, the optimal vector is the Weyl vector exactly when the Weyl vector lies in $L$. We give explicit formulas for the families $A_n$, $B_n$, $C_n$ and $D_n$, with elementary proofs, and the values for the exceptional types $E_6$, $E_7$, $E_8$, $F_4$ and $G_2$. Finally, we explain the coincidences between the minimal norms by the folding of Dynkin diagrams.
