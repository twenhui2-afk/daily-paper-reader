## Abstract
Over the field of complex numbers, we study root-of-unity limits of trigonometric qKZ connections associated with tensor products of $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ Verma modules and of their hypergeometric solutions. As the multiplicative step approaches a primitive $m$-th root of unity and the quantum-group and twist parameters approach $1$, the $m$-step qKZ transport yields, on every fixed-degree subspace,a differential connection. We show that this connection is the pullback of the trigonometric KZ connection under the power map $z\mapsto z^m$, with the KZ parameter rescaled by $m$, and establish formal and $\ell$-adic versions.   In degree one, with all highest weights equal to $1$, we determine the limits of normalized qKZ hypergeometric circle integrals in arbitrary angular chambers. The limits are expressed in terms of loaded hypergeometric integrals for the trigonometric KZ equations and explicit chamber-dependent upper-triangular matrices. We establish compatibility of the resulting distinguished fundamental matrices under inward analytic continuation, governed by the right weak Bruhat order,and under power maps, including composition of root orders.
