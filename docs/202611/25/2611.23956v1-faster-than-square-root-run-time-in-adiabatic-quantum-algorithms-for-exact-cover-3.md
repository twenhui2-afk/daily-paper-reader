## Abstract
By intertwining ideas from sequential and adiabatic quantum computing, we propose a quantum algorithm for the problem class Exact Cover 3, which is a sub-class of 3-satisfiability (3-SAT) problems and NP (nondeterministic polynomial time) complete. We find a quantum run-time which is significantly faster than the square-root run-time $N\to N^{1/2}$ of the Grover algorithm. Depending on the structure of the problem (e.g., connectivity of clauses), the run-time scaling may range from approximately third root $N\to N^{1/3}$ to nearly fourth root $N\to N^{1/4}$, possibly even faster.
