## Abstract
We investigate Euclidean self-orthogonality and the generalized Reed--Solomon (GRS) property for coefficient-vector codes generated by a monic polynomial $g$ and its shifts $xg,\ldots,x^{k-1}g$, of length $n=r+k$ and dimension $k$, where $°g=r$ and $3\le k\le r$. We give a complete classification, over any algebraically closed field, of the generators for which these codes are both self-orthogonal and GRS. Such generators have either distinct roots in a geometric progression satisfying explicit order and scalar conditions, or a single repeated root in certain positive characteristics. In particular, only finitely many generators occur for fixed $r$, $k$, and characteristic. We determine their maximal self-orthogonal lengths, show that the corresponding maximal-length codes are cyclic or negacyclic, and obtain exact generator counts over finite fields, together with the least fields of definition. In contrast, when $3\le k<r$, we prove that every algebraically closed field supports infinitely many pairwise monomially inequivalent self-orthogonal non-GRS maximum distance separable (MDS) codes in this family. Consequently, arbitrarily many such codes can be realized over a common finite extension of any prescribed finite field, while remaining inequivalent and non-GRS over its algebraic closure.
