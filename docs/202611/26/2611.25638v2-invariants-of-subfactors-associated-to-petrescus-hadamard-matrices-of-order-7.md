## Abstract
Petrescu's sixth-root $7 \times 7$ Hadamard matrix defines an irreducible, hyperfinite subfactor $N \subset M$ with Jones index 7. It is built from the spin model commuting square obtained from the Hadamard matrix by iterating Jones' basic construction. We prove that the standard invariant of this subfactor is minimal, i.e. the higher relative commutants consists of Temperley-Lieb algebras. Thus, the principal graphs of the subfactor are the Coxeter-Dynkin graph A$_\infty$. This solves a long-standing open problem. The main new idea is a congruence for the profile of the Hadamard matrix that restricts the order of rotation on the lowest-weight spaces in Jones planar algebra description of the standard invariant. Together with a uniform trace bound, this gives bounded annular multiplicities on a subsequence, which forces the principal graph to have norm at most two. We also show that the same result holds for most Hadamard matrices in Petrescu's one-parameter family. Specifically, the associated subfactors have Temperley-Lieb standard invariant at every transcendental phase and at every algebraic phase with residue $\pm1$ at a prime above three. In each fixed degree the exceptional set is finite and consists of algebraic numbers.   Our methods apply to several other families of Hadamard matrices of order $q >4$ as well, and we show that the associated subfactors have A$_\infty$ principal graphs. These include real Hadamard matrices of order $q\equiv4\pmod8$, Hadamard matrices over $\Q(i)$ of order $q\equiv2\pmod4$ and cube-root matrices of order $q$ with $3\mid q$ and $9\nmid q$. For order six Hadamard matrices, we obtain the same result at every transcendental phase in Diţă's family through Haagerup's matrix and at every algebraically independent pair of phases in Karlsson's two-parameter family.
