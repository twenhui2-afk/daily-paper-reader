## Abstract
We study three counting problems for lattice orbits in hyperbolic space: counting orbit points within a prescribed distance of a fixed closed geodesic or a totally geodesic hyperplane, and counting orbit points above a prescribed height at a cusp. For almost every base point, we obtain error bounds of square-root size up to logarithmic factors. For the geodesic and hyperplane counts, these bounds match the conjectured exponents and improve earlier results due to Good and Laaksonen. For these two problems, we adapt the method of maximal estimates developed by Chatzakos and Dimakis for hyperbolic lattice counting, formulating it in both a discrete and a continuous framework. For the hyperplane problem, we also obtain an asymptotic formula for the spatial second moment of the error term, involving an almost periodic function. For the cusp problem, the square-root bound can be deduced from work of Yu, which incorporates Schmidt's dyadic maximal argument. We present a proof, together with an $Ω$-result supporting the optimality of the exponent $1/2$ for at least one cusp of any group that is not essentially cuspidal.
