# Demazure–Lusztig type operators and correspondences on Kato Catalan varieties 

Taiyo Kodama ∗

Abstract 

We study the integral equivariant cohomology rings of Kato Catalan varieties XΨ associ-ated with arbitrary root ideals Ψ in type A. These varieties are smooth projective compact-ifications of the vector subbundles T ∗

> Ψ

B of the cotangent bundle of the complete flag variety 

B. Let K denote the product of the general linear group (or its maximal torus) with the loop rotation torus C×. We give an explicit presentation of H∗ 

> K

(XΨ; Z) with relations ex-pressed in terms of divided difference and Demazure–Lusztig type operators. Furthermore, for every simple reflection preserving Ψ ∪ (−Ψ), we construct a geometric realization of the associated Demazure–Lusztig type operator on H∗ 

> K

These cor-respondences yield a faithful graded action of the subalgebra of the degenerate affine Hecke algebra generated by these reflections and the polynomial subalgebra. For the maximal root ideal Ψ = ∆ +, our construction recovers the cohomological counterpart of the K-theoretic Steinberg correspondences of Cautis and Kamnitzer.

# 2 Combinatorics and the degenerate affine Hecke algebra 

2.1 Root ideals and reverse Hessenberg functions 

Let n ≥ 2 and set [ n] = {1, . . . , n }. Let G = GL n(C), with Borel subgroup B of upper triangular matrices and maximal torus T of diagonal matrices. Write g = gl n(C) and t = Lie( T ) for the Lie 3algebras of G and T , respectively. Let ϵi ∈ t∗ be the i-th coordinate character of T . The root system of ( G, T ) and its positive roots are ∆ = {ϵi − ϵj | i̸ = j}, ∆+ = {ϵi − ϵj | i < j }.

A subset Ψ ⊂ ∆+ is a root ideal if (Ψ + ∆ +) ∩ ∆+ ⊂ Ψ. Any root ideal Ψ is uniquely encoded by a reverse Hessenberg function , that is, a weakly increasing map r : [ n] → { 0, . . . , n − 1} satisfying 

r(j) < j for all j ∈ [n]. The bijection between root ideals and reverse Hessenberg functions is given by Ψr = {ϵi − ϵj ∈ ∆+ | i ≤ r(j)}, rΨ(j) = max ({i | ϵi − ϵj ∈ Ψ} ∪ { 0}).

The Weyl group W = NG(T )/T is identified with the symmetric group Sn, where the simple reflection si = ( i, i + 1) interchanges i and i + 1. 

Remark 2.1 (Hessenberg functions and reverse Hessenberg functions) . In combinatorics and the geometry of Hessenberg varieties, it is more common to encode a root ideal Ψ ⊂ ∆+ by a 

Hessenberg function , namely a weakly increasing map h : [ n] → [n] satisfying h(i) ≥ i for all 

i ∈ [n]. A Hessenberg function h determines a root ideal by Ψ h = {ϵi − ϵj ∈ ∆+ | j > h(i)}. The bijection between Hessenberg functions h and reverse Hessenberg functions r corresponding to the same root ideal is given by the equivalence i ≤ r(j) ⇐⇒ j > h(i), or explicitly by 

r(j) = max ({i ∈ [n] | h(i) < j } ∪ { 0}), h(i) = max {j ∈ [n] | r(j) < i }.

Although Hessenberg functions are more standard in the combinatorics literature, we follow Kamnitzer [Kam24, §2.1] in using reverse Hessenberg functions throughout this paper, as they allow us to write the defining lattice condition of Xr and the rank of each stage of the projective bundle tower directly in terms of r(j) (see Section 3).
