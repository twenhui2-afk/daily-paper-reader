# Self-Orthogonal MDS Principal-Ideal Codes 

## Yangcheng Li * Pingzhi Yuan †

School of Mathematical Sciences, South China Normal University Guangzhou 510631, Guangdong, China 

Abstract 

We investigate Euclidean self-orthogonality and the generalized Reed–Solomon (GRS) property for coe ffi cient-vector codes generated by a monic polynomial g and its shifts xg , . . . , xk−1g, of length n = r + k and dimension 

k, where deg g = r and 3 ≤ k ≤ r. We give a complete classification, over any algebraically closed field, of the generators for which these codes are both self-orthogonal and GRS. Such generators have either distinct roots in a geometric progression satisfying explicit order and scalar conditions, or a single repeated root in certain positive characteristics. In particular, only finitely many generators occur for fixed r, k, and characteristic. We determine their maximal self-orthogonal lengths, show that the corresponding maximal-length codes are cyclic or negacyclic, and obtain exact generator counts over finite fields, together with the least fields of definition. In contrast, when 3 ≤ k < r, we prove that every algebraically closed field supports infinitely many pairwise monomially inequivalent self-orthogonal non-GRS maximum distance separable (MDS) codes in this family. Consequently, arbitrarily many such codes can be realized over a common finite extension of any prescribed finite field, while remaining inequivalent and non-GRS over its algebraic closure. 

Keywords: Self-orthogonal code, MDS code, generalized Reed–Solomon code, principal-ideal code.

Classifications and GRS representations for cyclic and constacyclic codes include [8, 19, 24]. Recent cyclic non-GRS MDS constructions appear in [12, 15]. # 6 Conclusion 

We have classified the monic generators g for which Cg(r + k) is Euclidean self-orthogonal and GRS, with 3 ≤ k ≤ r.For fixed r, k, these generators form a finite set over each algebraically closed field. The classification determines their maximal self-orthogonal lengths and the associated shortening relations. Frobenius stability further gives their least coe ffi cient fields and exact counts over finite fields. For 3 ≤ k < r, the same model contains infinitely many monomial equivalence classes of self-orthogonal non-GRS MDS codes over every algebraically closed field. For every finite field Fq and every positive integer B, at least B such codes can be realized over a common finite extension of Fq, while remaining non-GRS and pairwise monomially inequivalent over Fq.
