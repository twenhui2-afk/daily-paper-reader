## Abstract
Let \(U=U_q(\mathfrak g)\) be the root-lattice form of the quantized enveloping algebra associated with a complex simple Lie algebra \(\mathfrak g\), over a field \(\mathbb{k}\) of characteristic zero, with \(q\in\mathbb{k}^{\times}\) not a root of unity. We determine the groups of automorphisms induced by algebra automorphisms of $U$ on its Cartan torus $U^0$ and its center $Z(U)$. These groups are   \[   \Aut_U(U^0)\cong\Hom(Q,\{\pm1\})\rtimes\Aut(Φ),   \]   \[   \Aut_U(Z(U))\cong\Hom((P/Q)[2],\{\pm1\})\rtimesΓ,   \]   where $Q$ and $P$ are the root and weight lattices, $Φ$ is the root system, $Γ$ is the automorphism group of the Dynkin diagram of $\mathfrak g$, and $(P/Q)[2]$ is the $2$-torsion subgroup of $P/Q$. The proof uses characters of finite-dimensional modules and Weyl-group   invariant fields to show that the automorphism of $Q$ determined by the toral restriction preserves $Φ$. Writing $ρ_0$ and $ρ_Z$ for the restriction maps to $U^0$   and $Z(U)$, respectively, we prove that   $\kerρ_0\subseteq\kerρ_Z$ and determine the quotient   $\kerρ_Z/\kerρ_0$.
