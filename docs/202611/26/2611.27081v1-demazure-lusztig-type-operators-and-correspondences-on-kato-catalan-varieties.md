## Abstract
We study the integral equivariant cohomology rings of Kato Catalan varieties $\mathscr{X}_Ψ$ associated with arbitrary root ideals $Ψ$ in type $A$. These varieties are smooth projective compactifications of the vector subbundles $T^*_Ψ\mathcal{B}$ of the cotangent bundle of the complete flag variety $\mathcal{B}$. Let $K$ denote the product of the general linear group (or its maximal torus) with the loop rotation torus $\mathbb{C}^\times$. We give an explicit presentation of $H^*_K(\mathscr{X}_Ψ;\mathbb{Z})$ with relations expressed in terms of divided difference and Demazure-Lusztig type operators. Furthermore, for every simple reflection preserving $Ψ\cup(-Ψ)$, we construct a geometric realization of the associated Demazure-Lusztig type operator on $H^*_K(\mathscr{X}_Ψ;\mathbb{Z})$ via correspondences. These correspondences yield a faithful graded action of the subalgebra of the degenerate affine Hecke algebra generated by these reflections and the polynomial subalgebra. For the maximal root ideal $Ψ=Δ^+$, our construction recovers the cohomological counterpart of the $K$-theoretic Steinberg correspondences of Cautis and Kamnitzer.
