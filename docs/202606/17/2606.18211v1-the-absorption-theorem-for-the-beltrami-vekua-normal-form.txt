# The Absorption Theorem for the Beltrami–Vekua Normal Form 

Daniel Alayón-Solarz ∗

June 2026 

Abstract 

The Beltrami–Vekua normal form assigns to every smooth first-order real planar elliptic system a complex equation w¯z − μw z + Aw + B ¯w = F by an explicit pipeline. A companion paper showed that the density Θ = |B| 2/(1 − | μ|2) dx dy and its total mass are invariants under multiplicative gauges w 7 → ϕw and orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms. The real system carries a larger symmetry: its unknowns may be recombined by any pointwise invertible real-linear substitution w = φv ′ + ψ¯v′, the complex gauges being the case ψ ≡ 0. We prove the absorption theorem: re-normalizing through the pipeline after any such substitution returns to the gauge orbit of the original equation, with a universal explicit gauge ˜φ = −iλ/ (φ − ψ),where λ is the spectral root of the structure polynomial. Hence every gauge–diffeomorphism invariant of the Beltrami–Vekua data — the density Θ, the pseudo-analytic mass, the zero locus of B — is an invariant of the underlying real elliptic system under its full natural symmetry groupoid. The proof is structural: pairing the unknown with a λ-eigencovector of the symbol’s canonical endomorphism gives an eigenpairing equation that is natural under substitutions and turns eigencovector rescalings into the gauge action; the pipeline normalization and the ambient form w = u + iv are sections of one eigencovector line whose ratio is the gauge. The result extends to orientation-reversing substitutions, so the invariant theory survives recombination by GL(2 , R)-valued matrix fields.

λ ∈ H+ simple. Pairing the unknown vector with any C1 left λ-eigencovector field ω of N produces a complex unknown w = ω · (u, v ) which satisfies a BV equation with explicit data — the eigenpairing equation E(ω) (Lemma 3.2). Three short lemmas then carry the entire argument: rescaling the eigencovector transforms E(ω) by exactly the gauge action (Lemma 4.1); recombining the unknowns transports the eigencovector and leaves E strictly invariant, E(ωS ) = E(ω), with the derivative contamination created by the substitution cancelling in one visible line (Lemma 4.2); and the pipeline computes E(ω0) in its own normalization ω0 of the eigencovector, as a direct consequence of the residual identity of [1] (Proposition 5.2). w = u + iv — indeed the canonical endomorphism of the realification is nothing but multiplication by λ (Lemma 5.3). Absorption is then formal: the substitution transports the ambient eigencovector to eS , the pipeline selects its own eigencovector ω′

Section 2 recalls the normal form, the residual identity — the single result imported from [1] — and the symmetry groupoid, and fixes the statement to be proved. Section 3 constructs the eigenpairing equation and proves the determinacy lemma that replaces jet realization. Section 4 proves gauge covariance and substitution naturality. # 7 Concluding remarks 

The proof re-founds the pipeline of [1] as the coordinate expression of a frame-independent spectral construction. The principal symbol defines the endomorphism N before any normalization is per-formed; its λ-eigencovector line is the invariant object; the eigenpairing equation E(ω) is the value of the construction at a section ω of that line; and the two normalizations that occur in practice — the pipeline’s spectral section ω0, with (ω0)2 = λ, and the ambient section e = (1 , i ) carried by the complex notation itself — differ by the universal gauge (4). The gauge group of [1] thus appears a priori, as the normalization ambiguity of an eigencovector, and the absorption theorem is the statement that the construction never chose a real frame to begin with. Three threads continue from here. First, the connection covector ϱ(ω) = ωx + λω y − ωP of (11) is the intrinsic form of the pipeline’s derivative terms, and Lemma 4.2 says it transforms ten-sorially along the eigencovector line; pairing the unknowns with a covector off the eigencovector line instead produces a framed Beltrami–Vekua class, where the corresponding connection term is the L-Wronskian of the frame and the pseudo-analytic mass admits a closed substitution-invariant extension. The present theorem identifies the pipeline with the algebraic frame-straightening of that class, settling the reformulation proposed there. Second, the spectral picture suggests degree-theoretic readings of the remaining invariants — the divisor of B as data of the eigenpairing, and the holonomy of A on multiply connected domains as the monodromy of the eigencovector normal-ization; with Corollary 6.2 these are now well-defined questions about real elliptic systems. Third, Corollary 6.4 extends the symmetry groupoid to GL(2 , R), and it is natural to ask for the corre-sponding extension on the diffeomorphism side — orientation-reversing coordinate changes — and for the joint classification.
