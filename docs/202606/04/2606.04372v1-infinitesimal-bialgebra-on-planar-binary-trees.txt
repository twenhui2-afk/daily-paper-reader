1.1. Motivation: infinitesimal compatibility on planar binary trees. Infinitesimal bialgebras were introduced by Joni and Rota as algebra–coalgebra structures in which the coproduct is a derivation with respect to the product [26]. In weight zero, this compatibility is expressed by 

∆(ab ) = a · ∆(b) + ∆ (a) · b.

Aguiar further developed infinitesimal Hopf algebras and emphasized that they are not merely formal variants of ordinary Hopf algebras: they are related to associative Yang–Baxter equations, pre-Lie structures, dendriform structures, and combinatorial Hopf algebras [1]. The present paper takes this infinitesimal point of view in a concrete tree-combinatorial setting. Instead of asking for a Hopf coproduct on planar binary trees, we ask for a coproduct which is compatible with the associative operation \ in the infinitesimal sense.

Planar binary trees already carry rich Hopf-theoretic structures. The Loday–Ronco Hopf alge-bra [29] relates them to associahedra, bracketings, and the Tamari order [34, 35, 36]. The Aguiar– Sottile analysis of this Hopf algebra describes a distinguished associative operation \, progres-sive trees, and a rooted-forest /binary-tree correspondence [2]. On the other hand, planar rooted forests form a classical source of combinatorial Hopf and infinitesimal bialgebras, including the Connes–Kreimer, Grossman–Larson, and related rooted-tree constructions [10, 11, 20, 27, 33]. Weighted infinitesimal bialgebras on rooted forests and their freeness properties were constructed in [37, 39]. The pre-Lie and operadic aspects of rooted trees are part of the background devel-oped in [7, 8, 9]. Further Hopf-algebraic developments for rooted trees and decorated rooted trees were obtained by Foissy and Holtkamp [13, 14, 15, 16, 23, 24]. Related algebraic struc-tures on or near planar binary trees include dialgebras, trialgebras, generalized bialgebras, mag-matic bialgebras, and Hopf operads [25, 30, 28]. Planar binary trees also appear in QED and renormalization-inspired Hopf algebras, as well as in the Hopf-theoretic study of bi-leveled trees and multiplihedra [4, 5, 17]. Noncommutative symmetric functions, free quasi-symmetric func-tions, and the Malvenuto–Reutenauer Hopf algebra provide another structural background for the Loday–Ronco setting [12, 19, 22, 31, 32]. Operated-algebra methods, including free operated al-gebras, Rota–Baxter type constructions, rooted-forest cocycles, and cocycle ∨-Hopf algebras, give a parallel framework for the constructions considered here [3, 6, 18, 21, 38, 40]. These two developments suggest a natural problem. Since forest concatenation has a weight-zero infinitesimal coproduct, and since the Aguiar–Sottile correspondence sends forest concate-nation to the product \ on planar binary trees, one expects a corresponding infinitesimal co-product on planar binary trees. However, this coproduct should not be confused with the Loday– Ronco Hopf coproduct, nor with the admissible-cut coproducts appearing in the Connes–Kreimer or Loday–Ronco Hopf settings. The purpose of this paper is to construct such an infinitesimal coproduct directly on planar binary trees and then to explain its universal and functorial meaning. 1.2.
