## 摘要
In this article，这篇论文研究 family 的 polynomials \[ P_n (s，z)=\sum_{k=0}^n s^{(k)} z^{n-k} \] 用于 s>0。

核心做法：We prove that its roots are simple，与 provide precise localisation 的 them in specific angular sectors。

主要结果：摘要给出了正向结果。

值得关注的点在于：这篇文章的价值在于把《Laguerre polynomials的On multiplicative perturbation》这个问题讲清楚。

## Abstract
In this article, we study the family of polynomials \[ P_n(s, z)=\sum_{k=0}^n s^{(k)} z^{n-k} \] for $s>0$. We prove that its roots are simple, and provide a precise localisation of them in specific angular sectors.
