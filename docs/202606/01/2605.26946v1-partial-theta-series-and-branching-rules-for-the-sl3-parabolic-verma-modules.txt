# Partial theta-series and branching rules for the sl (3) parabolic Verma modules 

E. Dotsenko ⋆

⋆ - NRC ”Kurchatov Institute”, 123182, Moscow, Russia

Abstract 

The monodromy of the sl (3) Casimir flat connection around root hyperplanes is stud-ied. For the computation of the traces of the root monodromy operators, acting on the parabolic Verma modules, we deduce branching rules w.r.t. the corresponding root sl (2)

subalgebras. We show that the traces of the monodromy operators are partial theta functions of special type. 

Key wors: monodromy of the Casimir connection, parabolic Verma modules, branching rules, partial theta functions. 1

1 Introduction 

In this paper we continue the study of the relation between monodromy operators of the Casimir connection and generalized theta series, initiated in [1]. In the case of 

sl (3) Casimir connection we produce partial theta series that correspond to three root monodromy operators. In order to compute these theta series we deduce the branching rules for the parabolic Verma modules. General properties of the branching rules for the parabolic Verma modules were studied in [2], [3]. The connection of the present work to the mock modular functions arising in the modern theory of invariants of knots and 3-manifolds [4], [5] and [6] is not developed. Let us recall some facts about Casimir connection. To a complex semi-simple finite-dimensional Lie algebra g one can associate two flat connections - Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov (KZ) flat connection ∇KZ and the Casimir flat connection ∇. The KZ flat connection appears naturally in the 2d WZW model [7]. The Casimir connection commutes with the KZ connection and shifts the twist parameters of ∇KZ , see [8], [9], [10]. Historically the theory of the Casimir connection was built by De-Concini, Felder-Markov-Tarasov-Varchenko [9] and independently by Millson and Toledano-Laredo [11], [12]. The generality of [9] allows to consider the Casimir connection associated to Kac-Moody Lie algebra (without Serre relations) g(C), where C is the Cartan matrix of general type. Let us also recall the recent development [13]. The paper organized as follows: in section 2 we recall the sl (3) Casimir connection and a method of constructing partial theta series, based on branching rules; in section 3 we deduce the branching rules for the Borel sl (3) Verma module, also we compute the corresponding partial theta series; in section 4 we deduce the branching rules for the parabolic sl (3) Verma modules, and the corresponding partial theta series are computed.
