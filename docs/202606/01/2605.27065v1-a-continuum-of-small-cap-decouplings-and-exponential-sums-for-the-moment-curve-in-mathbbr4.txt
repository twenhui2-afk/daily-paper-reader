A CONTINUUM OF SMALL-CAP DECOUPLINGS AND EXPONENTIAL SUMS FOR THE MOMENT CURVE IN R4

JACOB GLIDEWELL 

Abstract. We use the high-low method and wavepacket pruning to prove new small-cap decoupling estimates for the moment curve in R4. As an application, we verify a conjecture of Demeter regarding the L12 square-root cancellation of exponential sums associated with the moment curve in R4. This provides a continuum of square-root cancellation estimates that connects the Vinogradov MVT in R3 with a result of Bourgain, related to improving the best known estimate for the Lindelöf hypothesis.
