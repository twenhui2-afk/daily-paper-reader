MONOIDAL CATEGORIFICATION OF GENERALIZED CLUSTER ALGEBRAS AND CONJECTURES OF FRASER AND GLEITZ 

XIAO-JUAN AN, JIAN-RONG LI, AND YAN-FENG LUO †

Abstract. Hernandez and Leclerc introduced the notion of monoidal categorification of clus-ter algebras. We define similarly the notion of monoidal categorifications of generalized cluster algebras: an abelian monoidal category M is said to be a monoidal categorification of a gener-alized cluster algebra A if the Grothendieck ring of M is isomorphic to the upper generalized cluster algebra Aup , and if cluster monomials (resp. cluster variables) of A correspond to classes of real simple (resp. real prime simple) objects of M.Let ε be a root of unity such that ε2ℓ = 1 for some ℓ ∈ Z≥2. Denote by Cε the category of finite-dimensional modules of the restricted quantum loop algebra U res  

> ε

(Lsl k ) at root ε of unity, and let Cε,ξ be a full subcategory of Cε determined by a bipartition ξ ∶ I → {0, 1} of the Dynkin diagram. For k = 3, Gleitz conjectured that the Grothendieck ring of Cε,ξ is isomorphic to a generalized cluster algebra of rank 2 ℓ − 2, and that generalized cluster monomials correspond to classes of simple modules. This conjecture is a special case of a more general conjecture of Fraser. In this paper, we prove the first part of Gleitz’s conjecture. More precisely, for k = 3 and arbitrary ℓ ≥ 2, we prove that the Grothendieck ring of Cε,ξ is isomorphic to a generalized cluster algebra of rank 2 ℓ − 2. We also classify the real Kirillov–Reshetikhin modules of U res  

> ε

(Lsl 3) and obtain mutation sequences for the real Kirillov–Reshetikhin modules from the initial seed of the generalized cluster algebra. 

Key words : monoidal categorification of generalized cluster algebras; restricted quantum loop algebras at roots of unity; finite-dimensional modules

Fomin and Zelevinsky introduced the notion of cluster algebras [14] as an algebraic framework for studying total positivity in algebraic groups and canonical bases in quantum groups. As a natural generalization, Chekhov and Shapiro introduced the generalized cluster alge-bras which arise from the Teichm¨ uller spaces of Riemann surfaces with orbifold points [8]. The principal distinction between cluster algebras and generalized cluster algebras lies in the fact that the binomial exchange relations for cluster variables of cluster algebras are replaced by the multinomial exchange relations for cluster variables of generalized cluster algebras. In [8], Chekhov and Shapiro have shown that the generalized cluster algebras possess the remarkable Laurent phenomenon. Many other important results for cluster algebras extend to the general-ized setting, such as the finite-type classification, the existence and theory of g-vectors, and the construction of F -polynomials [8, 23]. The category M is called a monoidal categorification of A if the Grothendieck ring of M is isomorphic to A, and if (1) the cluster monomials of A are the classes of all the real simple objects of M;(2) the cluster variables of A (including the frozen ones) are the classes of all the real prime simple objects of M.We propose the following definition of monoidal categorification of generalized cluster algebras. 

Definition 1.1. Let A be a generalized cluster algebra and let M be an abelian monoidal category. A semistandard Young tableau is a Young tableau with weakly increasing rows and strictly increasing columns.
