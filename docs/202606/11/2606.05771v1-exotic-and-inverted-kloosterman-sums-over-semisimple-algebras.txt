DAQING WAN AND DINGXIN ZHANG 

Abstract. We introduce exotic Kloosterman sums and exotic inverted Kloosterman sums attached to non-commutative finite-dimensional semisimple algebras over a finite field Fq , and prove their reduction formulae to exotic Kloosterman and exotic inverted Kloosterman sums over commutative ´ etale Fq algebras. We then obtain square-root estimates for these sums; for inverted sums an explicit correction term may appear.

Deligne [ 1 ] and Katz [ 7 ] introduced “exotic” generalizations of Kloosterman sums, namely Kloosterman sums over finite ´ etale Fq-algebras. Already in Katz’s note on inverted Kloosterman sums, their exotic variants were considered [ 8 ].
