ARITHMETIC EXCEPTIONALITY OF GENERALIZED CHEBYSHEV POLYNOMIALS OF THE SECOND KIND 

DERYA ACAR, METİN AZMAZ, VURAL CAM, AND ÖMER KÜÇÜKSAKALLI 

Abstract. In this paper, we show that generalized Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind, associated with the root system A2, are not arithmetically exceptional. We achieve this by studying the norms of certain cyclotomic elements that admit a parametrization of finite fields.
