PGL 2 WEBS AT ROOTS OF UNITY 

VIJAY HIGGINS, BRYAN SILVA, AMY SOMERS, DAVID STEPHENS, SRIJAN SUNDAR, AND PARTH WOKHLU 

Abstract. We study webs on surfaces for the PGL 2 skein theory specialized at roots of unity. We construct the PGL 2 analogue of Chebyshev polynomials and use them to construct central elements in the skein algebra. We then focus on the cases of the 3-punctured sphere and the closed torus. We compute the Frobenius homomorphism for these two surfaces and show that the PGL 2 Frobenius homomorphism for the torus is not injective when the quantum parameter q2 is a root of unity of even order n.

Skein algebras are objects in quantum topology that are situated at the intersection of algebra and topology. They are closely related to character varieties, quantum groups, cluster algebras, mapping class groups, and TQFTs. The most well-studied skein algebra is the Kauffman bracket skein algebra Sq(Σ g,n ) of an oriented surface Σ g,n of genus g with 

n punctures. The algebra Sq(Σ) is spanned by framed link diagrams on Σ subject to the linear skein relations of equations (1) and (2), which are also the defining relations of the Temperley-Lieb ribbon category. The Kauffman bracket skein algebra may also be viewed as a generalization of the Jones polynomial to links in thickened surfaces.

In [BW16], it is shown that central elements in the Kauffman bracket skein algebra can be constructed by an operation known as threading (see Section 4), which can be informally described as replacing components of a link by elements represented by Chebyshev polynomials Tn when q2n = 1 . We will use this relationship when studying the Frobenius homomorphism for the PGL 2 skein algebra of the torus in Section 5.
