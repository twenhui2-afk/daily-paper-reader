## 摘要
We prove that Ehrhart h^*-polynomials 的 crown order polytopes are real-rooted，settling conjecture 的。

核心做法：用于 each integer r\ge3，crown order polytope on 2r elements 与 rank-r whirl base polytope have same h^*。

主要结果：h^*-polynomials 的 rank-r wheel 与 whirl base polytopes，as well as every convex combination 的 two，have。

值得关注的点在于：Their coefficient sequences are positive，unimodal，与 strictly ultra log-concave 的 order 2r-2。

## Abstract
We prove that the Ehrhart $h^*$-polynomials of crown order polytopes are real-rooted, settling a conjecture of Lundström and Saud Maia Leite. For each integer $r\ge3$, the crown order polytope on $2r$ elements and the rank-$r$ whirl base polytope have the same $h^*$-polynomial. The $h^*$-polynomials of the rank-$r$ wheel and whirl base polytopes, as well as every convex combination of the two, have $2r-2$ distinct negative roots. Their coefficient sequences are positive, unimodal, and strictly ultra log-concave of order $2r-2$.
