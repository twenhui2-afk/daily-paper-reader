Abstract 

We generalize discrete rational wave numbers (arXiv:2609.23233) to the octonionic rational wave numbers OZ over Cayley’s octonions O. Rather than assuming higher imaginary units, all rational wave number spaces CZ, HZ, OZ are derived constructively from two scalar primitives: the coordinate advance EZ and phase calibration E¯1. Their closure under pointwise multiplica-tion, inversion, and rational roots generates an infinite divisible Moufang loop on the sequence ring SO := OZ, terminating strictly at OZ via sedenionic zero-divisor excision. By Artin’s Theo-rem, pairwise compositions remain strictly associative, whereas three-wave interactions generate an intrinsic non-associative curvature quantified by the associator [ u, v, w ] = ( uv )w −u(vw ). We define a real-valued sequence inner product that bypasses the Jordan–von Neumann–Wigner ob-struction, establishing the orthonormality of the seven Fano coordinate primitives. We establish three principal theorems: (1) The Invariant Hexagonal Mesh Theorem, proving that cumula-tive integration exhibits a scale-invariant bifurcation at n = 6 governed by the root system of G2 = Aut( O) and its SU(3) stabilizer subgroup; (2) The Algebraic Phase Jitter Theorem, showing that Catalan parenthesization trees across non-Fano triads produce an irreducible zero-point phase fluctuation, yielding an algebraic origin for vacuum jitter; and (3) The Rational Loop Closure Theorem, proving that rational frequencies select closed finite Moufang sub-loops, while irrational frequencies yield topologically dense, non-closing trajectories. This provides an autonomous, discrete algebraic foundation for non-associative geometry and fundamental physical symmetries. 

Keywords: Octonions, Non-associative rings, Rational wave numbers, Moufang loops, Exceptional Lie group G2, Fano plane, Catalan trees, Division algebras, Invariant lattices.
