A COMBINATORIAL CRITERION FOR DETECTING ROOTS OF BERNSTEIN-SATO POLYNOMIALS 

QUAN SHI AND HUAIQING ZUO 

Abstract. We give a combinatorial criterion for detecting roots of Bernstein-Sato polynomials of ideals. As applications, we prove the strong monodromy conjecture for determinantal and Pfaffian ideals. Keywords. Bernstein-Sato polynomial, monodromy conjecture, determinantal ideal, Pfaffian ideal. MSC(2020). Primary 14F10; Secondary 13A35, 13C40, 32S40.

Recall the strong monodromy conjecture for determinantal ideals (resp. Pfaffian ideals) was already known when r = n (resp. r = ⌊N/ 2⌋), cf. [LRWW17, CSS13], while general cases remained open. The weak monodromy conjecture for determinantal and Pfaffian ideals was proved in [CZ25] and [CSXZ25], respectively, using a detailed analysis of monodromy zeta functions. Our proof of the strong monodromy conjecture uses a different approach, based on a characteristic p criterion of Mustat ¸˘ a, Takagi, and Watanabe [MTW05].
