DRINFELD PRESENTATION OF AFFINE iQUANTUM GROUPS VIA

MING LU AND XIAOLONG PAN 

Abstract. Affine quantum groups eU and affine iquantum groups eUı admit two com-plementary descriptions: a Serre-type presentation and a Drinfeld (current) presentation. While iquantum groups eUı are typically defined via the Serre presentation, their Drinfeld presentations are substantially more subtle and less well understood. In this paper, we give a conceptual derivation of the Drinfeld presentation of eUı starting directly from the Drinfeld presentation of eU, working within the framework of iHopf algebras. This ap-proach leads naturally to the definition of iquantum affinization associated with arbitrary Kac-Moody algebras g. We further show that, in this general setting, these iquantum affinizations can be realized as coideal subalgebras of quantum affinizations with respect to the Drinfeld coproduct. Finally, in the affine case, we describe the imaginary root vectors Θ i,r of eUı explicitly in terms of the root vectors ψ± 

> i,r

of eU, thereby extending the results of Li and Prze´ zdziecki for certain affine types of eUı.

1.1. Background. Quantum groups U, introduced independently by Drinfeld [Dr87] and Jimbo [Jim85], are Hopf algebras associated to arbitrary Kac-Moody algebras g. As in the setting of Kac-Moody algebras, quantum groups are classified by Dynkin diagrams. Associated with Satake diagrams ( I, τ ) (with only white nodes), Letzter [Let99] introduced the (quasi-split) iquantum groups Uı as coideal subalgebras of U, also known as the quantum symmetric pair coideal subalgebras. In this paper, we work with the Drinfeld double quantum group eU, in which the Cartan subalgebra is doubled, together with the universal iquantum group eUı introduced in [LW22a]. This framework provides the most natural setting for relative braid group symmetries; see [KP11, LW22b, WZ23, Z23]. We know eUı is a right coideal subalgebra of eU, and the pair ( eU, eUı) is called the (universal) quantum symmetric pair; see [Let99, Ko14, LW22a].

The affine quantum group eUv(g) admits two realizations: the Drinfeld-Jimbo realization (also called the Serre presentation) and the Drinfeld new realization (also known as the Drinfeld or current presentation); see [Dr88]. An explicit isomorphism between these two presentations was first stated by Drinfeld and later proved by Beck [Be94] and Damiani [Da12, Da15], using Lusztig’s braid group actions [Lus93]. The Drinfeld presentation of affine quantum groups has played a central role in many de-velopments in algebra, geometry, and category theory. This presentation has proven to be particularly crucial in the representation theory of affine quantum groups; see the survey [CH10] for further references. Frenkel and Reshetikhin [FR99] introduced the q-character morphism, which has since become a powerful tool for the study of representations. The Drinfeld presentation extends to quantum groups of Kac-Moody type eUv(g), giv-ing rise to the so-called quantum affinization (with central charges in this paper), usu-ally denoted by Dr eUv(bg); see [Jin98, GKV95]. In general, quantum affinizations are not quantum groups, except in the simplest case of affine quantum groups; rather, they are topological Hopf algebras (cf. [G07, H07, Da24]). These algebras are of particular in-terest, especially the quantum toroidal algebras arising when g is of affine type; see [GKV95, VV96, N01, H05, H07]. The Drinfeld presentations of the (universal) iquantum groups of split ADE type were initiated by the first author and Wang in [LW21], and were subsequently completed through a series of works [Z22, LWZ23, LWZ24, LPWZ25], reflecting the inherent com-plexity of Satake diagrams. Here, “split” refers to the case τ = Id in quasi-split Satake diagrams. The overall strategy is largely inspired by [Be94, Da12, Da15], with the rela-tive braid group actions [LW22b, WZ23] of eUı playing a crucial role in the construction. However, in these works, iquantum groups are treated as independent algebraic objects, and their embeddings into quantum groups are not taken into account. It is therefore natural to investigate the relationship between the Drinfeld presenta-tion of affine quantum groups and iquantum groups. This question was first studied by Prze´ zdziecki [P25] for split type A, and subsequently by Li and Prze´ zdziecki [LiP25] for split type BCD, as well as quasi-split type AIII [LiP26]. As an application, they introduced boundary q-characters for eUı and used these q-characters to study the rep-resentation theory of eUı. Their approach is to expand the Drinfeld generators of eUı in terms of the Drinfeld generators of eU via the natural embedding. This method relies on extensive calculations involving explicit reduced expressions for fundamental weights in the extended affine Weyl groups of classical types. The study of affine iquantum groups is very relevant to quantum integrable models with boundary conditions (cf. [EKP07]) such as open spin chains of XXZ type [Sk88], reflection equations [MRS03] and boundary affine Toda field theories (cf. [BB10]). 1.2. Goal. The goal of this paper is to derive the Drinfeld presentation of the affine iquan-tum groups eUı from that of the affine quantum groups eU, and to extend the framework of iquantum groups to the setting of iquantum affinization. Our approach is based on the theory of iHopf algebras initiated in [CLPRW25]. An iHopf algebra is a new associative algebraic structure defined on a Hopf algebra equipped with a Hopf pairing. When applied to a Borel subalgebra, this structure yields a re-alization of the iquantum groups eUı. Owing to the existence of two Borel subalgebras 3

arising from the two presentations of affine quantum groups, we develop two correspond-ing constructions of iHopf algebras. These constructions allow us to derive the Drinfeld presentation of eUı and to express its Drinfeld generators explicitly in terms of those of eU.Moreover, the second construction applies to arbitrary quantum groups of Kac-Moody type, leading to the definition of iquantum affinization. 1.3. The method developed in this paper, using iHopf algebras to estab-lish Drinfeld presentations, also applies to twisted affine types. In particular, the Drinfeld presentation of twisted Yangians can be obtained by degeneration from affine iquantum groups. This has previously been established case-by-case in [KLWZ25a, KLWZ25b, LZ26] via extensive Gauss decomposition computations. We expect that the Drinfeld presenta-tion of twisted Yangians can alternatively be derived directly from that of Yangians using the framework of iHopf algebras. As a distinguished class of quantum affinizations, quantum toroidal algebras (also known as double affine quantum algebras) have attracted significant attention. For their Schur-Weyl duality with elliptic Cherednik algebras (elliptic double affine Hecke algebras), see [VV96]; for geometric realizations, see [GKV95, N01]; and for their representation the-ory, see [H09]. For the iquantum toroidal algebras introduced in this paper, we conjecture that they likewise admit a Schur-Weyl duality with elliptic double affine Hecke algebras (cf. [BW18a, FL 3WW20, AP23] for the Schur-Weyl duality between iquantum groups and Hecke algebras). Moreover, we expect that these algebras also admit geometric real-izations, analogous to those for affine iquantum groups (cf. [FL 3W20, SW24]).
