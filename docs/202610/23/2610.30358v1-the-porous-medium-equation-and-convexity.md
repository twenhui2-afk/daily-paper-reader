## 摘要
We show that root convexity 的 pressure is preserved by porous medium equation if initial data is roo。

核心做法：This complements result 的 Daskalopoulos-Hamilton-Lee on root concavity 的 pressure。

主要结果：摘要给出了正向结果。

值得关注的点在于：这篇文章的价值在于把《Porous Medium Equation与Convexity》这个问题讲清楚。

## Abstract
We show that root convexity of the pressure is preserved by the porous medium equation if the initial data is root convex, positive, smooth, bounded below by a quadratic function and has bounded Laplacian. This complements a result of Daskalopoulos-Hamilton-Lee on root concavity of pressure.
