> Abstract. We show that root convexity of the pressure is preserved by the porous medium equa-tion if the initial data is root convex, positive, smooth, bounded below by a quadratic function and has bounded Laplacian. This complements a result of Daskalopoulos-Hamilton-Lee on root concavity of pressure.

When d = 1, the preservation of concavity was proved in [7] by B´ enilan-V´ azquez. In [15], Daskalopoulos-Hamilton-Lee proved that if the initial data v0 = v(x, 0) is root concave (in the sense that D2(√v0) ≤ 0) and smooth up to boundary, and satisfies the nondegeneracy condition v0 + |Dv 0|2 ≥ c > 0, then v(·, t ) is root concave up to boundary for all 0 ≤ t < ∞. This implies that the free boundary is smooth for all time ([14], [15]). Ishige-Salani proved in [17] that the convexity of free boundary breaks in short time. Chau-Weinkove proved in [13] that when d = 2, α-concavity ( vα is concave) is not preserved by the pressure for 0 ≤ α < 1/2 and 1 /2 < α ≤ 1 (0-concavity means that log v is concave) and Shen-Talla [19] proved this for d ≥ 3. Concavity or convexity properties of solutions to other parabolic problems have been considered also in [6,8,10,11,12,16,18]. It is natural to ask whether a classical solution exists and remains root convex if the initial data is smooth and root convex.
