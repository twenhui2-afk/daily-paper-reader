BOUNDARY AND INTERIOR OF ROOT SETS OF POWER SERIES 

JACOB KEWARTH 

Abstract. In this paper we study the set MM of roots of power series with coefficients in {0, ±1, ..., ±M } and constant coefficient 1. We prove that every nonreal root in the unit disk of a polynomial of this form is an interior point of MM . Moreover we establish a dimension formula and show the corresponding Hausdorff measure is finite and positive for a family of attractors parameterized by boundary parameters λ ∈

∂MM \ R. Finally we show that ∂MM has Lebesgue measure zero.

A central question has been to understand the interior points of MM .Since the intersection of MM with a ball of sufficiently small radius yields two real intervals, interior points cannot be dense in MM . Still computer images suggest that interior points are dense in MM \ R. For M = 1 this was conjectured by Bandt [Ba] and proven in [CKW]. A stronger conjecture is that all nonreal roots in D of polynomials with coefficients in EM and constant coefficient 1 are interior points of MM . For the M = 1 case [SX] proved there is a region of MM for which nonreal polynomial roots are interior points. This result was generalized to all M in [EJS] and [EJ] where it was shown that for M ⩾ 19, all nonreal polynomial roots are interior points. For smaller M , including M = 1, this remains unknown.
