NATURAL REPARAMETERIZATION AND CONTINUITY OF THE SOLUTION MAP FOR POLYNOMIALS 

ADAM PARUSI ´NSKI AND ARMIN RAINER 

Abstract. The optimal Sobolev regularity of the roots of a smooth curve of monic complex polynomials is W 1,q , for q ∈ [1 , dd−1 ), where d is the degree. This result is stable in the sense that the solution map from Cd coefficients to W 1,q roots is continuous. We prove that, after a natural Lipschitz repa-rameterization, the roots are Lipschitz and the map from Cd coefficients to reparameterization and reparameterized roots is continuous with respect to the 

W 1,q topology on the target spaces, for all q ∈ [1 , ∞). The result is based on a convergence and reparameterization theorem for Almgren’s Q-valued Sobolev functions. 

1. Introduction 

The optimal Sobolev regularity of the roots of monic complex polynomials with smooth coefficients was established in [PR16, PR18, PR20]. If the coefficients are functions of class Cd−1,1(I), where d is the degree of the polynomial and I a bounded open interval, then each continuous root is absolutely continuous, bounded, and 

λ′ ∈ Lq (I), for all q ∈ [1 , dd−1 ). In [PR24], stability of this result was proved, in the sense that the solution map which assigns to polynomials with coefficients in Cd(I)their unordered root tuple of Sobolev class W 1,q , for q ∈ [1 , dd−1 ), is continuous. In this note, we show that, by means of a natural Lipschitz reparameterization, these results extend to all values q ∈ [1 , ∞). Since there is no canonical ordering of the complex roots, we work with the space of unordered d-tuples and use Almgren’s theory of multivalued Sobolev functions (see [Alm00], [DLS11]). We view the symmetric product Sym d(C) = Cd/ Sd as the complete metric space ( Ad(C), d2) of unordered d-tuples [ z] = [ z1, . . . z d] of complex numbers, where
