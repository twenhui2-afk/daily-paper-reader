# Matrix roots of order n of a multivariate polynomial 

Jordan Noumi Bakop 1 and Yves Baudelaire Fomatati 21Department of Mathematics, Faculty of Science, Yaound´ e I University, Cameroon, jordanoumi@gmail.com .2Department of Mathematics, The University of Bamenda, Cameroon, fomatati.yves@uniba.cm .

Abstract 

In this paper, we construct the matrix nth root of a multivariate polynomial with coef-ficients in a field K which possesses an nth root of unity. Moreover, we explain why this concept is interesting. Finally, we give several examples to illustrate our construction. 

Keywords. Matrix factorization of polynomials, multivariate polynomials. 

Mathematics Subject Classification (2020). 15A23, 18A05. In the sequel, K is a field. 

# 1 Introduction 

Let f ∈ K[X1, ..., Xn] be a multivariate polynomial with coe ffi cients in K and indetermi-nates X1, ..., Xn. We further assume that K has a primitive nth root of unity. 

Definition 1.1. [1] A matrix square root of order m of a polynomial f ∈ K[X1, ..., Xn] is an m × m matrix A such that A2 = f I m

Im is the m × m identity matrix. 

In the sequel, we generalize this result for n ≥ 3. The case n = 2 was treated in [1]. The main result in [1] is that every polynomial f ∈ K[X1, ..., Xn] has a matrix square root .
