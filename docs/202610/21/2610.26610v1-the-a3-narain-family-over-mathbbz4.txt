THE A3 NARAIN FAMILY OVER Z4

RAJAE SAMMANI AND ABDELLAH SEBBAR 

Abstract. The code {000 , 220 , 202 , 022 } ⊂ Z34 realizes the split A3 root-weight gluing construction as ordinary Construction A, preserving the Lorentzian pairing and the positive energy form. For two hyperbolic pairs of A3 blocks, the twenty-two even self-dual gluing codes form seven block-monomial classes. Explicit metric and B-field representatives and theta series distinguish exactly seven T-duality classes in this family at central charge six; the one-pair family has two classes at central charge three. For arbitrary d, the chiral root system on either side is A3m 

> 1

, where m counts the words (2 ei, 2ei) in the gluing code. Exactly one two-pair class has no zero-B T-duality frame. Intersections of gluing codes determine finite shift orbifolds between the backgrounds, with the target code specifying the twisted-sector projections.

Proof. The reduction map is equivariant for the block-monomial group. Above each of the four coordinate planes are the two even graph lifts described in Proposition 4.2. The b = 0 graph contains an order-four word with exactly one nonzero coordinate, and the b = 2 graph does not. For the latter assertion, any order-four graph word has an odd a-coordinate; the corresponding entry of 2 Ja is nonzero in the opposite coordinate pair.
