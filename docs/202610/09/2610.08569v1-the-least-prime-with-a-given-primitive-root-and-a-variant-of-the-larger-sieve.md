## Abstract
For an integer $g$ not equal to $-1$ or a square, we study the least prime $p_g$ such that $g$ is a primitive root modulo $p_g$. We show that, assuming the Generalised Riemann Hypothesis, all such $g$ with $|g|\in[N,2N]$ have $p_g\leq(\log |g|)^{3.44}$ with at most $O((\log N)^{2.44})$ exceptions. This can be directly compared to the uniform bound $p_g\leq(\log |g|)^{19}$ proven recently by Fan and Pollack. To obtain our result, we prove a new variant of Gallagher's larger sieve, which may be of independent interest. In addition to our conditional result, we discuss other ``almost all" bounds for $p_g$ that hold unconditionally.
