## Root systems and Minimal RG flows to c = 1 CFTs 

Changrim Ahn †, Sara Kostadinovic ♯,♭ , and Francesco Ravanini ♯,♭ 

> †

Department of Physics, Ewha Womans University, Seoul 03760, Korea

> ♭

I.N.F.N., Sezione di Bologna, 40126 Bologna, Italy 

We derive exact massless S-matrices from algebraic root-system geometry and derive thermodynamic Bethe ansatz (TBA) equations that generate minimal renormalization group (RG) flows. We show how P. Dorey’s geometric construction, originally developed for massive S-matrices, can be extended to massless cases and prove that these new S-matrices straightforwardly satisfy the bootstrap equations. We focus on minimal 

Dn coset CFTs, which are c = 1 free-boson CFTs with compactification radius pn/ 2, and find their UV completions by analyzing the TBA. 

I. INTRODUCTION 

Symmetry plays a fundamental role in understand-ing both conformal field theories (CFTs), which de-scribe quantum field theories (QFTs) at renormalization-group (RG) fixed points, and integrable QFTs, obtained by adding special conformal-symmetry-breaking fields to CFTs. In this Letter, we show how mathematical sym-metry can determine the RG flows described by these QFTs, which interpolate between two RG fixed points, the UV and IR CFTs. In two dimensions, conformal symmetry provides ex-ceptionally powerful tools for characterizing fixed points through their central charges, operator spectra, and cor-relation functions. Consequently, researchers have stud-ied RG flows between two-dimensional CFTs using vari-ous analytical and numerical methods [1, 2]. One recent approach is based on exact thermodynamic Bethe ansatz (TBA) equations [3, 4] derived from mass-less S-matrices [5]. If a CFT deformed by a relevant operator maintains integrability, it can be described by factorizable S-matrices among massive excitations [6, 7]. The perturbed CFT remains integrable even with addi-tional irrelevant higher energy-momentum tensors, which modify the S-matrices by multiplying them by CDD-like scalar functions [8]. In the limit where the coefficient of the relevant de-formation vanishes, the integrable QFT is described by massless particles in two chiralities: right-moving ( R)and left-moving ( L). The S-matrices SRL ij between Ri

and Lj particles (with particle species indices i, j ) are non-trivial functions of the rapidity θ satisfying the crossing-unitarity condition, explicitly depending on the additional irrelevant operators, while scattering between particles of the same chirality, SRR ij and SLL ij , remains the same as those of the original massive S-matrices. RG flows are generated by thermodynamic Bethe ansatz (TBA) systems derived from these S-matrices. Even without detailed S-matrices, one can restrict UV-complete theories by imposing finite central charges on the plateau equations, which are the UV limit of the TBA. Solving these algebraic equations identifies UV-complete theories and three types of RG flows: minimal, diagonal, and saturated [5]. 1

Among these, the minimal flows interpolate from [ G]2

to [ G]1, where [ G]k ≡ G1 × Gk/G k+1 are coset CFTs and Gk denotes the WZW CFT at level k, with G =

A, D, E . Although these flows were already noticed based on guessed TBA systems in [10], they have re-mained mere conjectures since they have not yet been derived from exact S-matrices. The S-matrices that gen-erate the minimal RG flows are most important because 

S-matrices for other types of RG flows can be obtained straightforwardly from the minimal ones; the diagonal by SRL ij = 1 /S RR ij and the saturated by squares of the minimal S-matrices.

> V. SUMMARY AND DISCUSSION

In this Letter, we found the minimal S-matrices be-tween massless particles in two chiralities for [ Dn]1 coset CFTs deformed by a set of higher-energy-momentum ir-relevant operators. These CFTs correspond to c = 1 free-boson CFTs with compactification radius pn/ 2. UV-complete CFTs can be connected to each of these c = 1 CFTs by the minimal RG flows [ Dn]2 → [Dn]1 generated by the minimal S-matrices. In addition to the minimal RG flows, other allowed RG flows are also limited to the diagonal RG flows generated by the diagonal S-matrices with SRL ij = 1 /S RR ij , whose UV limit leads to generalized Dn parafermions [15], and the saturated flows generated by the square ( SRL ij )2 of the minimal ones. Although we have not addressed the UV completeness issue much here, we confirmed that the minimal, saturated, and diagonal solutions are the only possible solutions to the bootstrap equations for the 

Dn flows. We also proved the conjectured TBA systems [10] using inversion relations derived from the minimal 

S-matrices. Our main result is to find the minimal S-matrices by generalizing P. Dorey’s geometric construction of the S-matrices in terms of root systems and Weyl elements of the underlying Lie-algebraic symmetries. It will be interesting to classify all possible massless S-matrices in terms of the geometric construction for other integrable QFTs that exhibit RG flows.
