## Abstract
Reliable electrode-level degradation diagnosis is essential for accurate health estimation and non-destructive monitoring in advanced battery management. Diagnosis models developed under controlled laboratory conditions can fail when operating conditions change, while acquiring sufficient full-lifespan data in new target domains remains prohibitively time-consuming and practically unfeasible. Here, we propose a closed-loop domain generation framework to enable cross-domain battery health diagnosis under dynamic conditions. Physics-constrained domain generation is achieved through Variational Autoencoder-based latent extrapolation, which maps the source data and few-shot early-life target domain data into a shared latent space and generates cross-domain samples within physical boundaries. Bayesian optimization closes the generation-diagnosis loop by automatically calibrating the sampling, latent regularization, extrapolation, perturbation, and generation-volume hyperparameters using leakage-free development validation, thereby strictly excluding later-life target data from optimization and ensuring the quality and quantity of generated data. Numerical results demonstrate that, across four cross-domain cases, the proposed framework achieves average root mean square errors of 1.53%, 2.15%, and 2.02% for positive electrode active material loss, negative electrode active material loss, and lithium inventory loss, respectively, whereas traditional methods suffer from notably higher errors or even complete failure. This framework provides a general pathway for onboard and physics-consistent electrode-level degradation diagnosis, independent of downstream diagnosis models.
