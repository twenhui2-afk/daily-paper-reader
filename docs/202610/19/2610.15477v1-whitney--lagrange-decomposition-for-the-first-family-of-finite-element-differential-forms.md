## Abstract
We derive polynomial bubble duality in barycentric coordinates and construct explicit nodal bases for the first family of finite element differential forms. Logarithmic coordinates give an elementary proof of the Whitney--bubble decomposition. The Hessian of negative entropy defines a metric whose normalized Hodge star maps bubble generators to complementary-degree polynomial forms. This gives a direct geometric description of the known dualities and proves nondegeneracy of their integral pairings. Combining Whitney forms with shifted Lagrange polynomials then gives bases dual to tangential point evaluations in every dimension and order. The construction extends to scalar nodes that are unisolvent on every face. The interpolant is independent of the roots and commutes with face restriction.
