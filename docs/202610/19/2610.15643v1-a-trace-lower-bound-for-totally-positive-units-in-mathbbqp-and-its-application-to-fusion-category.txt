# 1 Introduction 

A fusion category over the complex field C is a semisimple finite tensor category. Fusion cate-gories have two important concepts: the Frobenius-Perron dimension and global (or categorical) dimension. Structure and properties of fusion categories with restrictions on their Frobenius-Perron dimension are widely studied, see [5, 6]. However, unlike the Frobenius-Perron dimension, currently, we even don’t know the structures of fusion categories with a prime global dimension. Recently, progress was made in topics related to the global dimension of fusion category, see [12, 14, 15, 20] and the references therein for details. It was asked in [19, Question 3.8] whether a spherical braided fusion category of prime global dimension is pointed (i.e., all simple objects have Frobenius-Perron dimension 1) or equivalent to Fib ⊠ Fib σ, where Fib and Fib σ are the Fibonacci fusion category and its Galois conjugate with global dimensions being 5+ √52 and 5−√52 ,respectively, which was proved in [14]. Moreover, spherical fusion categories of prime dimension 

p = 2 q + 1 are pointed if q is also an odd prime [14, Theorem 5.5]. Meanwhile, non-simple modular fusion categories of global dimension p2 are completely classified in [20, Theorem 4.14]. Let C be a spherical fusion category of global dimension p, where p is prime. One of the main techniques used in [14, Theorem 5.5] is by showing the Frobenius-Perron dimension (generally, formal codegrees [11]) of C equals to p · u for some algebraic unit u ∈ Q(ζp) (see [14, Lemma 5.1]). Together with the Siegel’s trace theorem [17, Theorem III], the relation of formal codegrees [12, Proposition 2.10] shows that C must be pointed if p = 2 q + 1, where q is an odd prime. Inspired by the result of [14], in this paper, we continue to consider the structure of C for the case p = 4 q + 1, where q is also an odd prime. However, it turns out that the Siegel’s trace bound is insufficient to show the pointedness of C for p = 4 q + 1, see [14, Example 5.7] and Lemma 4.1 below. Notice that the formal codegrees of fusion categories are totally positive [11], so we first pay our attentions to 1arXiv:2610.15643v1 [math.QA] 11 Oct 2026 give a new lower bound for totally positive algebraic units in Q(ζp). Namely, we consider the trace of totally positive units ε ∈ U + 

> K

for any subfield Q ( K ⊆ L := Q(ζp)+.Our first main result is stated as follows.

The following corollary is a direct result of [4, Proposition 1], [9, Theorem 4.4] and [7, Propo-sition 3.1]. 

Corollary 1.2. Let p = 4 q +1 be a prime integer, where q is an odd prime integer. Let L = Q(ζp)+,and let K 6 = Q be any totally real subfield of L. If 

• 2 is a primitive root modulo q, or 

• p = 4 q + 1 < 222 and p 6 = 29 ,then for any non-trivial totally positive unit 1 6 = ε ∈ U +

The paper is organized as follows. In section 2, we review some basic notions of algebraic number theory and fusion category. In section 3, for primes p = 4 q + 1, we first prove the trace inequality under the assumption that every totally positive unit is a square in L = Q(ζp)+ (Theorem 1.1), and then we show that the trace inequality always holds under certain restrictions (Corollary 1.2). In section 4, we apply to trace the bound to classify spherical fusion category of prime global dimension (Theorem 1.3).
