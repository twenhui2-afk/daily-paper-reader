A UNIVERSAL FRAMEWORK FOR HOPF ALGEBRAS IN COMBINATORICS 

FARID ALINIAEIFARD AND STEPHANIE VAN WILLIGENBURG 

Abstract. A universal framework for Hopf algebras in combinatorics provides a unified understanding of combinatorial objects. To establish such a framework, we dualize the theory of combinatorial Hopf algebras developed by Aguiar, Bergeron, and Sottile, obtaining the category of rooted Hopf algebras. An object in this category is a graded connected Hopf algebra equipped with a group-like element, called a root, in its completion. These roots dualize the character theory of combinatorial Hopf algebras and provide a natural way to extend Bernstein and raising operators to other rooted Hopf algebras. The resulting constructions extend Schur, Hall–Littlewood, and Macdonald functions to rooted Hopf algebras. As an application, we develop these constructions for symmetric functions in noncommuting variables.

Schur functions are central objects in many branches of mathematics, including representation theory, algebraic combinatorics, and algebraic geometry, and also arise in physics. Their generalizations include Hall–Littlewood and Macdonald polynomials, as well as analogues in other Hopf algebras such as the immaculate basis of noncommutative symmetric functions [3], a lift of Schur Q-functions to the peak algebra [12], and Schur functions in noncommuting variables [2]. These related families admit constructions using Bernstein and raising operators. We introduce the category of rooted Hopf algebras, dual to the category of combinatorial Hopf algebras of Aguiar, Bergeron, and Sottile [1], and define universal expressions using a collection of scalar series C and a group-like element h. Suitable choices of C and h recover the functions and operators mentioned above. Operator constructions of symmetric functions provide important foundations for this framework.
