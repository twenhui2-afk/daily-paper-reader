# Colored Khovanov homology theories at prime roots of unity 

## You Qi, Louis-Hadrien Robert, Joshua Sussan and Emmanuel Wagner October 2, 2026 

Abstract 

We equip Khovanov’s colored gl p2q-homology groups with a degree-2 derivation. Over a field of char-acteristic p ą 2, the derivation specializes to a p-nilpotent differential, which leads to a categorification of colored gl p2q-homology at a 2pth root of unity.

# 1 Introduction 

Khovanov homology [Kho00] categorifies the Jones polynomial where the quantum parameter is assumed to be generic. This was extended to a categorification of the colored Jones polynomial in [Kho05]. Beliakova and Wehrli [BW08] showed that this construction could be done over Z (with 2 inverted). Von Merkl [vM25] showed that various constructions in [Kho05] are isomorphic. These categorifications all yield finite-dimensional homology theories. We will not discuss various interesting categorifications of the colored Jones polynomial which have infinite-dimensional homologies. The WRT 3-manifold invariant may be defined as a sum of colored Jones polynomials evaluated at a root of unity. An approach to categorifying this invariant was introduced by Khovanov [Kho16] who suggested looking for p-differentials on algebraic structures defined over a field of characteristic p. These are derivations B such that Bp “ 0.arXiv:2610.01122v1 [math.GT] 1 Oct 2026 2

In [QRSW24, QRSW23, QRSW25], the authors studied sl p2q-actions on equivariant Khovanov–Rozansky homology. Working over a field of characteristic p, one obtains a p-differential B “ f where f is a generator of sl p2q.In this paper we show the following. 

Theorem. Colored Khovanov homology (colored by tilting modules over the distribution algebra for sl 2

in characteristic p) may be equipped with a p-differential yielding a categorification of the colored Jones polynomial at a 2pth root of unity. In particular, when the colors of the components are less than p, we obtain a categorification of the standard colored Jones polynomial at 2pth roots of unity. This colored homology theory is functorial. An approach to a categorification of the (colored) Jones polynomial using gl p´ 2q-homology was con-structed in [QS22, QRSW21] and is orthogonal to this work.
