THE BORISOV–NUER CONJECTURE AND ROOT DECOMPOSITIONS IN EVEN UNIMODULAR LORENTZIAN LATTICES 

HAOWU WANG AND SHAOYUN YI 

Abstract. We prove that every vector of II 8n+1 ,1, with n ≥ 1, is a sum of two roots if and only if n = 1 or 2. This settles the Borisov–Nuer conjecture and implies the existence of Ulrich line bundles for every very ample polarization on a complex unnodal Enriques surface. For vectors of negative norm, we express the number of root decompositions as a Fourier coefficient of a vector-valued theta series, reducing existence to positivity. The main analytic difficulty is to obtain estimates uniform over lattices of unbounded determinant. We address this by combining a reduction based on primitive sublattices of codimension one and least determinant with explicit bounds for theta norms and Fourier coefficients of cusp forms.

1. Introduction and main results 

Let X be a smooth complex projective variety of dimension d, and let H be a very ample divisor. A vector bundle E on X is Ulrich with respect to H if 

Hi(X, E(−jH )) = 0 for all i and 1 ≤ j ≤ d. 

Ulrich bundles provide linear resolutions of the corresponding sheaves in projective space and enter the study of Chow forms and cohomology tables; see [13, 3]. Their existence is a basic problem, and the rank-one case imposes particularly strong restrictions on the divisor classes of the variety. Let Y be a complex Enriques surface. We equip its numerical divisor lattice with the negative of the intersection form. For lattices, ⊕ denotes the orthogonal direct sum; for abelian groups, it denotes the ordinary direct sum. Thus Num( Y ) = Pic( Y )/⟨KY ⟩ ∼= U ⊕ E8 ∼= II 9,1, (D, E ) = −D · E, 

where · denotes the usual intersection product; see [9] and [11, §3]. Here U is a hyperbolic plane and E8 is the positive definite root lattice. We write D2 = ( D, D ), so a very ample class H has H2 < 0. This convention, with one negative direction, will be used throughout. The surface Y is unnodal if it contains no smooth rational curves. Borisov and Nuer [4, Proposition 2.1] proved that a line bundle OY (D) on such a surface has vanishing cohomology in every degree if and only if D2 = 2. Consequently, OY (D) is H-Ulrich if and only if (D − H)2 = ( D − 2H)2 = 2 .

Setting r = D − H and s = 2 H − D, we obtain the equivalent condition 

H = r + s, r2 = s2 = 2 . (1.1) Borisov and Nuer established this decomposition for multiples of a Fano polarization and proposed the following numerical conjecture [4, Conjecture 2.2 and Theorem 2.4], stated here in our sign convention. 

Conjecture (Borisov–Nuer) . Every vector of II 9,1 is a sum of two vectors of square 2.

Aprodu and Kim [1] related this numerical condition to the images of moduli spaces of polarized Enriques surfaces in K3 moduli spaces. Their Conjecture 1.3 is a direct consequence of Borisov–Nuer’s conjecture. We prove the conjecture and study the same question for all even unimodular Lorentzian lattices.
