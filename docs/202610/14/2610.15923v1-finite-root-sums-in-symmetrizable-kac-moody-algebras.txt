FINITE ROOT SUMS IN SYMMETRIZABLE KAC–MOODY ALGEBRAS 

ANDREW DOUGLAS 1,2,3

Abstract. Let g be a symmetrizable Kac–Moody algebra with root system Φ. A subset S ⊆ Φis closed if α, β ∈ S and α + β ∈ Φ imply α + β ∈ S. For finite and affine Kac–Moody algebras, this closure property is known to extend from pairs to arbitrary finite sums that are roots. We prove that every closed subset of the root system of any symmetrizable Kac–Moody algebra is closed under arbitrary finite sums that are roots. The proof uses real-root strings and simultaneous separation of positive imaginary roots, together with an addition lemma for fundamental imaginary roots. As an application, we extend a necessary condition for wideness of regular subalgebras from finite and affine types to all symmetrizable Kac–Moody algebras: the symmetric closure of the roots associated to a wide regular subalgebra is the entire root system. 

1. Introduction 

Let g be a symmetrizable Kac–Moody algebra with Cartan subalgebra h, root system Φ ⊂ h∗,and invariant symmetric bilinear form ( · | · ). Let A be the generalized Cartan matrix of g, of size 

ℓ × ℓ. Fix a set of positive roots Φ +, with corresponding simple roots Π = {α1, . . . , α ℓ}. Write Φre = {α ∈ Φ : ( α | α) > 0}, Φim = {α ∈ Φ : ( α | α) ≤ 0}

for the real and imaginary roots, and let Φ +im = Φ im ∩ Φ+. A subset S ⊆ Φ is closed if α, β ∈ S and 

α + β ∈ Φ imply α + β ∈ S.For finite type [1, Lem. 3.2] and affine type [2, Prop. 3.4], this closure property was shown to extend from pairs to arbitrary finite sums that are roots. We say that Φ has the finite-sum closure property , or simply finite-sum closure , if for every closed subset S ⊆ Φ, 

β1, . . . , β n ∈ S, η := β1 + · · · + βn ∈ Φ =⇒ η ∈ S. 

Our main theorem shows that every symmetrizable Kac–Moody algebra has the finite-sum closure property. 

Main theorem. Let g be a symmetrizable Kac–Moody algebra with root system Φ. Then Φ has the finite-sum closure property. 

The proof proceeds by induction on the number of summands. The cases involving real roots use real-root strings and negative-pairing arguments. For imaginary roots, we use Marquis’s simul-taneous separation lemma [4, Lem. 3.4] and an addition lemma for fundamental imaginary roots. Together, these handle imaginary summands of both signs. As an application, we extend a necessary condition for wideness of regular subalgebras from finite and affine types [1, 2] to all symmetrizable Kac–Moody algebras. We show that if s is a wide regular subalgebra and T is its associated subset of roots, then [T ∪ (−T )] = Φ ,

where brackets denote closure.

In this section, we extend the finite-sum closure property from semisimple and affine Kac–Moody algebras [1, 2] to all symmetrizable Kac–Moody algebras.

Theorem 4.1 (Necessary condition for wideness) . Let g be a symmetrizable Kac–Moody algebra, and let s ⊆ g be a regular subalgebra with associated subset T ⊆ Φ. Then 

s wide =⇒ [T ∪ (−T )] = Φ .

Proof. Set S := [ T ∪ (−T )]. As in [2, Thm. 3.5], S is symmetric and closed. Suppose S̸ = Φ. Then Π ⊈ S: indeed, if Π ⊆ S, every positive root is a finite sum of simple roots, so Theorem 3.1 gives Φ+ ⊆ S, and symmetry gives Φ − ⊆ S, contradicting S̸ = Φ. Remark 4.2 (Wideness criteria) . If h ⊆ s, then [2, Thm. 3.3] and Theorem 4.1 give Π ⊆ cl re (T ∪ (−T )) =⇒ s is wide =⇒ [T ∪ (−T )] = Φ .

Thus the real-closure condition is sufficient for wideness, while [ T ∪ (−T )] = Φ is necessary. 

Remark 4.3 (A remaining question) . If h ⊆ s and [ T ∪ (−T )] = Φ, must s be wide? An affirmative answer would make the necessary condition in Theorem 4.1 sufficient as well.
