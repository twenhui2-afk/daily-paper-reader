## Abstract
The Okubo algebra brings together ternary gradings, integral root frames and rank-three geometry. We examine how these structures extend to the complex Exceptional Periodicity algebras. With $N=4(n+1)$, a tetracode character gives eight nonzero homogeneous components of dimension $t_n=8n+3+2^{4n}$. On a tip, a compatible grading has zero component of dimension $8n-5$ and eight nonzero components of dimension $1+2^{4n-3}$. The arithmetic behaves differently: for $n\geq2$, the lattice $D_N^+$ has no full-rank orthogonal frame of unscaled $A_2$ lattices. We exhibit failures of the Jacobi and Jordan identities and determine the derivations and full automorphism group of the ambient algebra above level one. We also give a Clifford-module proof, with the normalization fixed by root products, of the published realization of EP tips as special Vinberg $H$-algebras. This identifies a positive real form and yields explicit formulas for its cone, cubic, Cholesky coordinates and rational dual invariant. The cone is symmetric exactly at $n=1$. Its determinant-one hypersurface belongs to the known $L(8n,1,0)$ series, whose $r$- and $c$-maps give homogeneous scalar manifolds for supergravity theories with eight real supercharges.
