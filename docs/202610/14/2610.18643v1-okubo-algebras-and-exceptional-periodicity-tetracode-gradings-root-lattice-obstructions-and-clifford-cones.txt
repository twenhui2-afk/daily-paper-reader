ALESSIO MARRANI AND DANIELE CORRADETTI 

Abstract. The Okubo algebra brings together ternary gradings, integral root frames and rank-three geometry. We examine how these structures extend to the complex Exceptional Periodicity algebras. With N = 4( n + 1), a tetracode character gives eight nonzero homogeneous components of dimension tn = 8 n + 3 + 2 4n. On a tip, a compatible grading has zero component of dimension 8 n − 5 and eight nonzero components of dimension 1 + 2 4n−3. The arithmetic behaves differently: for n ≥ 2, the lattice D+ 

> N

has no full-rank orthogonal frame of unscaled A2 lattices. We exhibit failures of the Jacobi and Jordan identities and determine the derivations and full automorphism group of the ambient algebra above level one. We also give a Clifford-module proof, with the normalization fixed by root products, of the published realization of EP tips as special Vinberg H-algebras. This identifies a positive real form and yields explicit formulas for its cone, cubic, Cholesky coordinates and rational dual invariant. The cone is symmetric exactly at n = 1. Its determinant-one hypersurface belongs to the known L(8 n, 1, 0) series, whose 

r- and c-maps give homogeneous scalar manifolds for supergravity theories with eight real supercharges. 

1. Introduction 

Octonions link exceptional Lie algebras, Jordan algebras, lattices and projective geom-etry. The para-octonion and Okubo products retain the composition norm but change the multiplication. We work with these three eight-dimensional models; the wider class of nonunital composition isotopes lies outside this comparison. Three features of the compact Okubo algebra guide the discussion. Its complexification has a Pauli grading by F23, with eight one-dimensional nonzero components. In the integral Petersson model of [5], twelve nonzero idempotents generate an A42 sublattice of E8, and the tetracode supplies its glue. The classical Magic Star, meanwhile, has six 27-dimensional tips, each carrying an Albert product once a basepoint is chosen. These features involve different choices of field, multiplication and integral data. We keep those choices explicit throughout. Exceptional Periodicity (EP) extends the root-coordinate description of E8 to N = 4( n+1) coordinates [26, 27, 28, 30, 29, 20]. Above level one the resulting anticommutative algebra fails the Jacobi identity. We study its character gradings, root lattice and tip products. The tetracode gives a balanced grading at every level, whereas an unscaled full-rank A2

frame occurs only at level one. Even there, the character kernel and the idempotent lattice are different: their root systems are D4 and A42, respectively. For the geometry we start from the published realization of the tip as a special Vinberg 

H-algebra [28, Section 8, Remark 8.2 and Appendix A]. Definition 8.4 fixes the product, and Propositions 8.8 and A.2 give its unit and associative trace form. In Section 8 we match this realization to our frame and derive the Clifford relations directly from the root product. A single root calculation fixes their scalar coefficient. The grading and arithmetic constructions separate above level one. Root-additive brack-ets continue to respect the tetracode character, and the [4 , 2, 3] 3 code gives balanced fibres at every level. By contrast, all norm-two vectors then lie in DN . Orthogonal A2 subsystems 20 A. MARRANI AND D. CORRADETTI 

need disjoint three-coordinate supports, which rules out a full-rank unscaled frame. The Jacobi and Jordan witnesses show directly where the classical algebraic identities fail. The associative trace and a root calculation give a Clifford proof of the published H-algebra realization. This provides a positive real form of the tip and places its Newton cubic in the known L(8 n, 1, 0) series. Cholesky coordinates identify the positive cone, and the rational dual invariant gives the corrected attractor inversion above the magical level. The next questions concern compatibility and classification. How do the character grad-ings split into orbits under the full automorphism group? Which admit nonuniform refine-ments or are compatible with an integral order and the transported real structure? The spinorial coset also leaves room for integral and boundary constructions beyond norm-two frames. Any incidence geometry built from that boundary would need its own points, lines and incidence theorem.
