# Asymptotic spectral properties and extreme eigenvalues of sample correlation matrices from nonstationary high-dimensional VAR processes 

Ruihan Liu ∗ and Chen Wang †

Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science, The University of Hong Kong 

Abstract 

We study the limiting spectral distribution and extreme eigenvalues of the sample correlation matrix ˆR from a high-dimensional vector autoregressive (VAR) processes. Depending on whether roots of the characteristic polynomial of given VAR lie on (non-stationary) or outside (super-nonstationary) the unit circle, ˆR presents different types of asymptotic spectral properties. Under both nonstationary and super-nonstationary regimes, the empirical spectral distribution of ˆR degenerates in probability to a point mass at zero. The rescaled extreme eigenvalues of ˆR are asymptotically equivalent to the eigenvalues of a finite-dimensional symmetric random matrix determined by the characteristic roots. Moreover, under super-nonstationarity, ˆR degenerates in proba-bility to a matrix with finite rank. 

# 1 Introduction 

The study of high-dimensional sample correlation matrix has attracted huge attention. As the entries of the sample correlation matrix are standardized, the statistics based on the sample correlation matrix share an advantage of scaling invariance.
