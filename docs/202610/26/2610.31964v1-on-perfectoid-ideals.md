## Abstract
In this paper, we study perfectoid ideals using the reduction modulo $p$ of perfectoid rings. We show that perfectoid ideals correspond to ideals in the reduction modulo $p$ on which Frobenius is surjective. We establish the existence of universal $p$-adic power series whose evaluations, together with their compatible $p$-power roots, generate perfectoidizations of ideals. In the special case of the perfectoidization of $\mathbb{Z}_p^{\mathrm{cycl}}\langle t^{1/p^{\infty}}\rangle/(t-1)$, we describe a linear algebra algorithm for computing finite approximations of such generators. We illustrate this by an explicit approximation modulo $8$ of an element $σ\in (t-1)_{\mathrm{perfd}}\subseteq \mathbb{Z}_2^{\mathrm{cycl}}\langle t^{1/2^{\infty}}\rangle$ which admits compatible sequences of $2$-power roots and satisfies $σ\equiv t-1\pmod{2}$.
