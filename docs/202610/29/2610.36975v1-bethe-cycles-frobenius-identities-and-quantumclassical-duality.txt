BETHE CYCLES, FROBENIUS IDENTITIES, AND QUANTUM/CLASSICAL DUALITY 

PETER KOROTEEV 

Abstract. We study Frobenius action on the trigonometric Ruijsenaars–Schneider sys-tem associated with cotangent bundles to complete flag varieties. The construction con-tinues the program initiated by Etingof and Varchenko and developed in quantum K-theory by the author and Smirnov. We explain how the monodromy eigenvectors recently constructed by Tarasov and Varchenko fit the quantum/classical duality between Bethe equations and tRS level sets. At a root of unity, all partial shifts cancel in the Newton– Adams combinations of the tRS Hamiltonians, leaving precisely the operators obtained by Frobenius action. This proves compatibility of the joint eigenvalue problem with Frobenius action in every rank and at every root order.

1.1. Motivation and the Frobenius Program. Recent work on the arithmetic properties of integrable connections has uncovered relations between Frobenius action and classical spectral geometry. Etingof and Varchenko [EV1,EV2] initiated this study for periodic pencils of flat connections and their p-curvature. In quantum K-theory, the author and Smirnov [KS] showed that full-cycle difference transport at roots of unity has the spectrum of quantum multiplication at powered parameters. The p-adic approximations of vertex functions studied by Smirnov and Varchenko [SV] provide another instance of the arithmetic structure of these enumerative functions. In the present paper we shall examine Frobenius action from the point of view of the trigonometric Ruijsenaars–Schneider (tRS) system [RS, R] associated with the cotangent bundle to the complete flag variety T ∗ Fl n. The goal is to understand how the recently con-structed monodromy eigenvectors of Tarasov and Varchenko [TV] enter the quantum/classical duality between Bethe equations and tRS level sets. Throughout the paper, the difference operators act on Kähler parameters, while the equivariant parameters a1, . . . , a n determine their spectrum. Our construction is also motivated by the qKZ/tRS correspondence of the author and Zeitlin [KZ]. In that work a weighted sum of components of a geometric qKZ solution pro-duces a tRS eigenfunction, which is identified with a vertex function in Theorem 5.11. This

The cancellation used in the original dynamical/tRS correspondence occurs in the auxil-iary variables of the vertex representation. Kähler shifts insert tautological classes into the phase, and the identities underlying the scalar eigenvalue equation remove internal twisted differences. Integration over a compatible cycle annihilates these differences. In a finite orbit sum, consecutive weighted terms cancel by reindexing, leaving a boundary term proportional to Bp,α − 1 in each internal direction. Here Bp,α is the product of the phase factors around that coordinate cycle. It is a rational function of the equivariant parameters ai, the Kähler parameters zr, and the tautological roots xr,u , with q and ℏ fixed; see (3.48).
