Abstract 

We construct Lax pairs with a spectral parameter for a family of models of interacting tops associ-ated with the BC n root system. The construction uses the parametric BC n associative Yang–Baxter equations, which involve both quantum R-matrices and boundary K-matrices satisfying the reflec-tion equation. Special cases yield spin generalizations of the elliptic Calogero–Inozemtsev model. We also show how quantum Hamiltonians of interacting tops, combined with the Polychronakos freezing prescription, reproduce recently introduced anisotropic quantum long-range spin chains of Haldane–Shastry–Inozemtsev type.

41 Introduction 

In recent years, there has been renewed interest in spin generalizations of integrable particle systems at both the classical and quantum levels [3, 11, 12, 20, 24, 25, 31, 37, 38, 45, 46, 47, 52, 54, 58, 60, 65]. This interest is motivated by the deep connections of these systems with the representation theory of quantum algebras, the geometry of spectral curves, gauge theories, and other areas. Recent works [46, 47] introduced a parametric generalization of the type-B associative Yang–Baxter equation algebra originally proposed in [32]. This made it possible to construct R-matrix-valued Lax representations for the elliptic BC n Calogero–Inozemtsev system and to derive the Hamiltonian of an elliptic anisotropic long-range quantum chain of Haldane–Shastry–Inozemtsev type associated with the BC n root system. In this paper, we use the BC n associative Yang–Baxter equation algebra and its matrix representations to construct classical integrable 1 systems that extend the elliptic BC n Calogero–Inozemtsev system to various spin models.

Purpose of the paper. The main contribution of this paper is a classical Hamiltonian construction of generalized interacting tops associated with the BC n root system. It extends the A-type construction of [25] using the R- and K-matrix data developed in [46, 47]. The latter works establish the relevant BC n associative Yang–Baxter identities and their matrix representations, and use them to construct 

R-matrix-valued Lax pairs and long-range quantum spin chains. Here these data are used to define a system with dynamical classical spin variables. For arbitrary n and N , we construct an explicit 2nN × 2nN Lax pair and a compatible Hamiltonian structure, as stated in Theorems 1 and 2.

Finally, we discuss applications to quantum long-range spin chains of Haldane–Shastry–Inozemtsev type [26, 29]. We obtain quantum Hamiltonians of interacting tops by direct quantization in the fundamental representation of the Lie algebra gl N . Applying the Polychronakos freezing prescription [50] then yields a family of quantum Hamiltonians, including (1.19), which was derived in [46, 47] using R-matrix-valued Lax pairs. These results provide a construction of a new family of (presumably) integrable quantum elliptic spin chains, although a rigorous proof of integrability remains a separate problem. Their relation to interacting tops may also facilitate the computation of higher Hamiltonians.

# 8 Conclusion 

We have constructed BC n generalizations of the model of interacting tops using parametric associative Yang–Baxter identities involving R- and K-matrices. The generalized model has two spin-orbit com-ponents. We have derived its Hamiltonian structure and Lax equation. Its reductions include one-orbit models, N = 1 spin Calogero–Inozemtsev systems and the spinless Calogero–Inozemtsev model. We have also obtained explicit XYZ and XXZ interactions, quantized the Hamiltonians in the fundamental representation, and described their relation to long-range spin chains. 56 The main classical special cases are summarized in Figure 1. The two operations N = 1 and A = B

commute, as shown by the two paths to the one-spin model. The rank-one branches include the moment constraints tr( Aii ) = g and the Cartan quotient described in Sections 5 and 6.
