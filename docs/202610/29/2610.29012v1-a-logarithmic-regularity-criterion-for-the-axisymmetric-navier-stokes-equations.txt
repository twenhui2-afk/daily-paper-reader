A LOGARITHMIC REGULARITY CRITERION FOR THE AXISYMMETRIC NAVIER–STOKES EQUATIONS 

STAN PALASEK 

Abstract. For axisymmetric solutions of the Navier–Stokes equations in R3, the cir-culation Γ = ru θ obeys a maximum principle, and the resulting a priori bound on Γ

is scale-invariant. Nonetheless, the global regularity problem remains completely open. The sharpest known regularity criteria in terms of the circulation are slightly subcritical, namely |Γ| ≲ (log r−1)−k near the symmetry axis; Lei and Zhang proved regularity for 

k = 2 , and Wei later lowered the exponent to k = 3 /2. In this note, we strengthen the criterion further to k = 5 /4. The argument follows the usual route of energy estimates for the quantities ωr /r and ωθ /r , but now with a sharper bound on the vortex stretching term in the equation for ωr /r , in which the loss is proportional only to the square root of the number of scales between the inner vortex core and the far field.
